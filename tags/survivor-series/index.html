  • Confirmed WWE November 4, 2026

    How a WarGames match works

    WarGames puts two teams inside one cage built over two rings. Wrestlers enter on a timer, one side gets a numbers advantage, and the real match starts only when everyone is in. WWE's version has no roof and allows pinfalls.
