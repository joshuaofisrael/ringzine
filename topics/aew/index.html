AEW

AEW news and results: Dynamite, Collision, and pay per views. Every story is labelled Confirmed or Rumor and links its sources.

  • Confirmed AEW November 12, 2026

    Motor City Machine Guns beat FTR at the buzzer to win the Best of 5 series

    Alex Shelley rolled up Dax Harwood in the final seconds of Match 5 on Dynamite, giving the Motor City Machine Guns a 3 to 2 series win and a shot at the AEW World Tag Team titles after Full Gear.

  • Confirmed AEW November 12, 2026

    Tommaso Ciampa retains the AEW National title against Mike Bailey, Ilja Dragunov is next at Full Gear

    Ciampa beat Speedball Mike Bailey in his first defence on Dynamite, then Ilja Dragunov came out and confirmed their National title match for Full Gear on Saturday.

  • Confirmed AEW November 12, 2026

    Marina Shafir makes Mercedes Moné tap out on Dynamite days before their title match

    Shafir and Jon Moxley beat Moné and Jay White in a mixed tag match when Shafir caught the AEW Women's World Champion in the Brutalizer, ahead of their Full Gear title match.

  • Confirmed AEW November 12, 2026

    Queen Aminata keeps the TBS title and gets another three way at Saturday's Tailgate Brawl

    Aminata beat Skye Blue and Hyan on Collision. AEW then made a new three way TBS title match against Persephone and Maya World for the Full Gear Tailgate Brawl show.

  • Confirmed AEW November 12, 2026

    AEW Full Gear 2026: the full card, start time and how to watch on Saturday

    Full Gear is on Saturday 14 November in Phoenix, with Will Ospreay against Kyle Fletcher for the AEW World title and five more championship matches after Wednesday's go home show.

  • Confirmed AEW November 11, 2026

    AEW runs a three-hour Dynamite and Collision tonight as the last stop before Full Gear

    AEW's official preview says the combined show airs live at 8 p.m. ET from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, with a mixed-tag Full Gear preview and a World Title face-off between Ospreay and Fletcher.

  • Confirmed AEW November 11, 2026

    Tony Khan books FTR vs Motor City Machine Guns in Best of 5 Match 5 on Dynamite

    The AEW president said the series is tied 2-2 after last weekend, and that the decider airs on the Wednesday three-hour Dynamite and Collision show.

  • Confirmed AEW November 9, 2026

    Tommaso Ciampa nicknames the AEW National Championship "Ruby" after Collision title win

    In a backstage video after beating Andrade El Idolo on the November 7 Collision filmed on the Chris Jericho Cruise, Ciampa calls the National Championship Ruby and says he wants more AEW gold.

  • Confirmed AEW November 9, 2026

    AEW sets Thanksgiving-night Collision for November 26 after Thanksgiving Eve Dynamite in Omaha

    AEW will tape a special Thanksgiving edition of Collision on November 25 in Omaha and air it on Thursday, November 26, at 8 p.m. ET on TNT and HBO Max.

  • Confirmed AEW November 8, 2026

    Tony Khan books Ciampa vs Speedball Bailey for the AEW National title on 11 November

    Tony Khan announced Tommaso Ciampa will defend the AEW National Championship against Speedball Bailey on the special three-hour Dynamite/Collision on Wednesday 11 November, days after Ciampa won the title from Andrade El Idolo.

  • Confirmed AEW November 8, 2026

    Tommaso Ciampa Wins AEW National Championship From Andrade on Jericho Cruise

    Tommaso Ciampa defeated Andrade El Idolo on the November 7 episode of AEW Collision to become the new AEW National Champion.

  • Confirmed AEW November 7, 2026

    AEW Full Gear upgrades trios title match to Texas Death rules

    AEW confirmed on Dynamite that Hangman Page, Bandido and Brody King will defend the World Trios titles against Swerve Strickland, Austin Creed and Kofi under Texas Death rules at Full Gear, with Ospreay vs Fletcher also set.

  • Confirmed AEW November 7, 2026

    AEW Dynamite Blood & Guts tops NXT in Wednesday ratings

    Tony Khan thanked viewers after AEW Dynamite Blood & Guts, saying the show had excellent viewership including AEW's most 18-49 viewers on TBS in years, as F4W/WON reported Dynamite beating WWE NXT head-to-head.

  • Confirmed AEW November 6, 2026

    AEW Full Gear: Mercedes Moné defends against Marina Shafir

    AEW booked Mercedes Moné versus Marina Shafir for the AEW Women's World Championship at Full Gear on 14 November, after a confrontation at Blood and Guts.

  • Confirmed AEW November 1, 2026

    AEW Collision results, October 31, 2026: Triangle of Madness retain, Dragunov wins

    The Triangle of Madness kept the Artist of Stardom titles against Trinity's team, Ilja Dragunov beat The Beast Mortos, and Tommaso Ciampa earned an AEW National Title shot for next week.

  • Confirmed AEW October 31, 2026

    AEW Fright Night Collision preview, October 31, 2026: The Cut vs. The Conglomeration

    Halloween Collision from St. Louis features The Cut against The Conglomeration, an Artist of STARDOM title defense by Triangle of Madness and Ilja Dragunov against The Beast Mortos.

  • Rumor AEW October 31, 2026

    AEW Fright Night Dynamite draws 620,000 viewers against the World Series, report says

    Wrestlenomics figures reported by Fightful put the October 28 Dynamite at 620,000 TBS viewers and a 0.14 rating among adults 18 to 49, both slightly down from the week before.

  • Confirmed AEW October 30, 2026

    How an AEW Blood and Guts match works

    AEW's Blood and Guts puts two rings under one roofed cage. Wrestlers enter on a clock, there are no pinfalls, and a team wins only when an opponent submits or surrenders.

  • Confirmed AEW October 30, 2026

    AEW Full Gear 2026 adds Anarchy in the Arena: Death Riders vs. The Cut

    Jon Moxley challenged The Cut to Anarchy in the Arena at Full Gear on Fright Night Dynamite. Here is the Full Gear card so far for November 14 in Phoenix.

  • Confirmed AEW October 29, 2026

    AEW books Ospreay vs. Fletcher for Full Gear and a Collision card

    Tony Khan made Will Ospreay versus Kyle Fletcher official for the AEW World Championship at Full Gear. Saturday's Collision has a posted card, and a women's Blood and Guts match is set.

  • Confirmed AEW October 29, 2026

    AEW Fright Night Dynamite results, October 28, 2026: Aminata wins the TBS title

    Queen Aminata beat Persephone for the TBS Championship, Darby Allin kept the TNT Championship against Josh Alexander, and the Motor City Machine Guns took Match 3 from FTR.

  • Confirmed AEW October 28, 2026

    AEW Fright Night Dynamite preview: Allin vs. Alexander and two more title stakes

    Darby Allin defends the TNT title against Josh Alexander, Persephone faces Queen Aminata for the TBS title, and FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns wrestle Match 3 tonight in St. Louis.

  • Confirmed AEW October 25, 2026

    AEW Collision results, October 24, 2026: FTR tie the series, women's Blood and Guts made

    FTR beat the Motor City Machine Guns to tie their Best of 5 series, Jay White beat Kyle O'Reilly, and AEW confirmed a women's Blood and Guts match for November 4.

  • Confirmed AEW October 22, 2026

    AEW Dynamite results, October 21, 2026: Dragunov wins debut, men's Blood and Guts set

    Ilja Dragunov beat Mike Bailey in his AEW debut, Queen Aminata earned a TBS title shot, and the Don Callis Family accepted a men's Blood and Guts match for November 4.
