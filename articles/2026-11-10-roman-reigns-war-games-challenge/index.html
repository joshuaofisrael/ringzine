Roman Reigns and The Bloodline challenge The 946 to WarGames at Survivor Series

By RingZine

Raw ended with Jacob Fatu's return, a brawl with The 946 and Roman Reigns laying down a WarGames challenge for Survivor Series.

Last updated November 10, 2026

Monday's Raw closed with Roman Reigns accepting a challenge from Royce Keys and then issuing his own: The Bloodline against The 946 in WarGames at Survivor Series, according to Sports Illustrated.

How the main event segment played out

Reigns, the WWE World Heavyweight Champion, came to the ring first and Keys followed. After a short exchange, Keys admitted he had not come alone and The 946 joined him. Reigns replied that he had not come alone either.

The broadcast cut backstage to The Usos, Solo Sikoa and a returning Jacob Fatu setting fire to the 946 van. They then hit the ring and a brawl broke out. The Bloodline stood tall after Fatu drove Carmelo Hayes through a table with a splash. As The 946 retreated, Reigns took the microphone, accepted Keys' challenge and called for WarGames at Survivor Series later this month.

How the night built to it

Earlier, The 946 had attacked Rey Fenix, Big Cass and Chad Gable straight after their Intercontinental contender match, and Keys used that moment to warn that the same would happen to Reigns' family unless Reigns met him and acknowledged him. Stadium Rant lists The 946 as Keys, Carmelo Hayes, Cruz Montana, Lucien Price and Bronco Nima.

The rest of the Survivor Series picture

Raw also confirmed Survivor Series: USA vs Mexico, with Cody Rhodes captaining the American side opposite Rey Mysterio. CM Punk asked to join Team USA and Rhodes accepted, while Mysterio later recruited Penta. Becky Lynch challenged Liv Morgan for the Women's World Championship after teaming with IYO SKY to beat Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez, and Adam Pearce made that match official for Survivor Series.

What to watch

WarGames depends on numbers, so the key question now is who fills out each side. The Bloodline showed four alongside Reigns on Monday, while The 946 has five listed members. Expect the next few weeks of Raw to settle the teams and the advantage rules before the event.
