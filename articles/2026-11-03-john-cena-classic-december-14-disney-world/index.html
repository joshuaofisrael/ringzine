John Cena Classic Set for December 14 as WWE Plans Disney World Event

By RingZine

WWE has announced a December 14 John Cena Classic at Disney World, part of a two-day event partnership with Disney.

Last updated November 3, 2026

WWE has set the John Cena Classic for December 14 at the ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex near Orlando, with a second wrestling event planned for the following day. The announcement emerged during the November 2 Raw broadcast and was detailed in November 3 coverage of the WWE and Disney partnership. Rather than being a standard weekly episode at an arena, the Classic is being positioned as a special live Monday Night Raw event from the Disney sports complex.

The December 14 programme is designed to feature leading Raw and SmackDown performers competing against talent from NXT. Cageside Seats reports that Club WWE members will be able to vote in real time for the night's top performer. That voting component is an unusual element of the announced format, but the reporting does not establish that viewers will determine match winners. The distinction matters: audience recognition and the outcomes of staged matches are not interchangeable.

The next day, December 15, WWE plans an Avengers: Doomsday-themed Worlds Collide event involving WWE, NXT and AAA talent. The tie-in is scheduled ahead of the film's December 18 theatrical release. This makes the two-day programme more than a single tribute event: it combines WWE's roster structure, an outside wrestling promotion and a Disney film marketing campaign within the same venue and week.

WWE and Fanatics also plan a two-day WWE World fan experience. Reported activities include merchandise, autograph opportunities and photo sessions. Co-branded Avengers: Doomsday products, including title belts, are expected to be sold at the fan experience, WWEShop.com and selected Disney stores. Those sales plans are confirmed elements of the announcement, but no revenue forecast or ticket-sales projection is provided in the cited report.

For fans considering attending, the published ticket timetable is concrete. The combo-ticket presale is scheduled for November 12, followed by general sales on November 13. Prospective buyers should verify the official ticketing page for eligibility, package inclusions and any subsequent changes. A presale date does not establish ticket availability, pricing or whether the event will sell out.

The announcement also extends an existing relationship. Cageside Seats notes that WWE and Disney announced a partnership in 2025 that made ESPN the exclusive US home of WWE premium live events for ESPN Unlimited subscribers. The December events add a physical destination and a film crossover to that commercial relationship. The John Cena Classic itself is reported to air live on Netflix as a special Raw, so viewers should distinguish its announced streaming arrangement from the broader ESPN premium-event deal.

Cena's involvement carries symbolic weight because his name is attached to a new event rather than merely a one-off appearance. Yet the confirmed details concern the date, venue, participating brands and fan-voting element. They do not justify guessing which wrestlers will win, how long the format will last or whether it will recur annually. Those remain matters for future WWE announcements.

The clearest immediate implication is a new date on the December wrestling calendar: a WWE showcase on the 14th, followed by an Avengers-linked crossover on the 15th. For attendees and streaming viewers alike, the useful information is the precise scheduling and the different identities of the two programmes, not speculation about unannounced match results.
