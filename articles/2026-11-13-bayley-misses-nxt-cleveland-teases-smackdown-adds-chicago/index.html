Bayley pulls out of tonight's NXT Cleveland show and hints at a SmackDown appearance

By RingZine

Bayley said she will miss the NXT live event at the Agora Theatre on 13 November because she has business to handle, adding a SmackDown logo to her post. She is still booked for NXT Detroit and now NXT Chicago.

Last updated November 13, 2026

Bayley will not be at tonight's NXT live event in Cleveland. In a social media post on Thursday, she told fans at the Agora Theatre she would not make the show because she has "some business to handle", Fightful reported. The post included a SmackDown logo, which points to an appearance on Friday's SmackDown instead.

Her weekend schedule

  • Friday 13 November, NXT Cleveland (Agora Theatre): no longer appearing
  • Saturday 14 November, NXT Detroit: still scheduled
  • Sunday 15 November, NXT Chicago: newly added

The Wrestling Observer says the announcement came in an Instagram story and confirms the Detroit and Chicago dates. It adds that a Saturday match in Detroit would be her first in NXT since a three day tour in May.

Why SmackDown

Fightful says Bayley has aligned with Charlotte Flair, Tatum Paxley and Alexa Bliss in their fight with Jade Cargill, B-Fab, Michin and Fatal Influence. WWE has not announced Bayley for tonight's show, so the logo is a hint rather than a booking. John Cena and Tiffany Stratton lead the advertised card; see our SmackDown preview.
