AEW Fright Night Dynamite draws 620,000 viewers against the World Series, report says

By RingZine

Wrestlenomics figures reported by Fightful put the October 28 Dynamite at 620,000 TBS viewers and a 0.14 rating among adults 18 to 49, both slightly down from the week before.

Last updated October 31, 2026

AEW's Halloween week Dynamite held above 600,000 viewers despite going head to head with the World Series. Wrestlenomics reports the October 28 episode averaged 620,000 viewers on TBS, Fightful said. AEW has not published its own figures, so treat these as reported numbers.

The numbers

Episode Viewers on TBS Adults 18 to 49 rating
October 28 (Fright Night) 620,000 0.14
October 21 661,000 0.15

That is a drop of 41,000 viewers week to week, based on the two reported averages.

The competition

The episode aired against Game 5 of the World Series between the Milwaukee Brewers and the Tampa Bay Rays, according to Fightful. Dynamite was also simulcast on HBO Max, and no viewership data is available for that stream, so the TBS figure does not capture the full audience.

What was on the show

AEW's own recap says Fright Night Dynamite saw Queen Aminata win the TBS Championship and Kyle Fletcher earn a shot at AEW World Champion Will Ospreay at Full Gear. Our Fright Night Dynamite results cover the full show, and the Fright Night Collision preview covers tonight's follow up.
