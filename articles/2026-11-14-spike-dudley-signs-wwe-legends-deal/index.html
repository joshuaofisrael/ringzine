Spike Dudley signs a WWE Legends deal after the trademark standoff ends

By RingZine

The former ECW and WWE star announced the contract on Facebook and said new merchandise is coming. He had abandoned his own trademark application for the name in early November.

Last updated November 14, 2026

Spike Dudley has signed a WWE Legends contract. The former ECW and WWE star, whose real name is Matthew Hyson, announced it on Facebook on Friday, F4W reported, saying he would "keep you posted with merch."

From trademark fight to deal

The signing ends what looked like a possible dispute over his ring name. According to F4W, Dudley had filed to trademark "Spike Dudley" for merchandise earlier this year, and WWE was granted a 90 day extension at the end of September to potentially oppose it. Dudley then abandoned his application at the start of November, and WWE filed its own application for the name on 6 November.

What it means

Legends deals usually cover merchandise and appearances. In a video, Dudley said the deal "really means a lot" but gave few details about any projects, POST Wrestling reports. F4W says the contract includes new Spike Dudley WWE merchandise.

Who Spike Dudley is

POST Wrestling recaps his career: he broke through in ECW in 1996 as "Little" Spike Dudley alongside The Dudleys, won the ECW tag titles twice with Balls Mahoney, and later held the WWE Tag Team, European, Cruiserweight and Hardcore titles before his release in 2005. He also worked in TNA as Brother Runt. F4W says he is 56 and now works in finance.
