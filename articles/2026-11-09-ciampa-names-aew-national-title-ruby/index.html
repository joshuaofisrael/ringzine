Tommaso Ciampa nicknames the AEW National Championship "Ruby" after Collision title win

By RingZine

In a backstage video after beating Andrade El Idolo on the November 7 Collision filmed on the Chris Jericho Cruise, Ciampa calls the National Championship Ruby and says he wants more AEW gold.

Last updated November 9, 2026

Tommaso Ciampa has given the AEW National Championship a personal nickname after winning the title from Andrade El Idolo on the November 7 episode of AEW Collision, PWMania reported on November 8. In a backstage video released after the match, Ciampa calls the championship Ruby.

Collision that night was filmed aboard the Norwegian Jewel during the Chris Jericho Cruise. The title change itself was already reported; the new detail is what Ciampa said afterward.

What Ciampa said

According to PWMania, Ciampa presented himself as a collector of championships, pointed to his earlier TNT Championship reign and his new National Championship win, and said he intends to pursue further titles, including the AEW World Championship. The report quotes him introducing Ruby as the name for the National Championship. That is an on-screen character statement, not confirmation that AEW has officially renamed the championship in its records.

What is confirmed

PWMania identifies Ciampa as the fifth holder of the National Championship since its introduction in November 2025, and says AEW released the backstage video after Collision. Those points support the immediate news: a champion has attached a personal identity to the belt while promoting broader ambitions. They do not establish future opponents, dates for additional title matches or whether his stated goal becomes a booking decision.

What to watch next

Whether AEW adopts the Ruby nickname in graphics or commentary remains open. Official match announcements and subsequent television appearances will show how the angle is used. The current evidence supports the new nickname and Ciampa's expressed ambitions, without treating a promo boast as a booked result.
