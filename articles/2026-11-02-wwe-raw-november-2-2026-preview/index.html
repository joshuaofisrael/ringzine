WWE Raw preview, November 2, 2026: card and how to watch on Netflix

By RingZine

WWE goes to Brooklyn for the final Raw before Crown Jewel, with Chad Gable defending the Intercontinental Championship against Ethan Page and John Cena back on the show for a special announcement.

Last updated November 2, 2026

WWE closes out the road to Crown Jewel tonight with Raw from the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, and WWE's event listing confirms John Cena and Roman Reigns are both booked for the show. Raw goes out at a special time because WWE tapes SmackDown right after it, Fightful reported.

Announced card

  • WWE Intercontinental Championship: Chad Gable (c) vs. Ethan Page
  • Six person tag team match: Penta, Je'Von Evans and Dragon Lee vs. The Vision (Austin Theory, Bron Breakker and Bronson Reed)
  • Solo Sikoa vs. Royce Keys
  • John Cena makes a special announcement
  • Roman Reigns addresses Sami Zayn ahead of Crown Jewel

Oba Femi, Women's World Champion Liv Morgan, Sol Ruca, Dominik Mysterio and Men's Money in the Bank winner Penta are also listed to appear, according to Netflix's Raw preview.

Gable defends against Page

Gable carries the Intercontinental Championship into Brooklyn against Ethan Page, one of five confirmed matches and segments for the night, per WWE's official card. The six person tag match doubles as a tune up for Crown Jewel, where Breakker and Reed are set to defend the World Tag Team Championship against Penta and Evans. RingZine covered that Crown Jewel booking and the rest of the lineup in the Crown Jewel match card story.

Solo Sikoa and Royce Keys round out the match card. Both men came through the Raw go home stretch toward Survivor Series, and WWE has not announced any stipulation beyond a straight singles match.

Cena returns, Reigns speaks to Zayn

John Cena is advertised for his first Raw appearance since his retirement, and he is set to make what WWE and Netflix both call a special announcement, Netflix's preview said. Neither source has revealed the subject of the announcement.

World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns is also booked to address Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn in Brooklyn. Zayn kept his title on the October 26 Raw, as RingZine reported in the Raw results from that night, and he now heads toward a champion versus champion match with Reigns at Crown Jewel on Saturday, November 7.

How to watch

Raw streams live on Netflix at a special start time of 6 p.m. Eastern and 3 p.m. Pacific from the Barclays Center, Fightful and Netflix's own preview both confirm. The earlier slot clears room for WWE to tape the next SmackDown once Raw wraps.

What's next

Crown Jewel airs Saturday, November 7, with Zayn defending the Undisputed WWE Championship against Reigns and the World Tag Team Championship on the line, full details in RingZine's Crown Jewel match card. RingZine will post full Raw results after tonight's show, and keeps every WWE Raw episode tracked alongside Netflix scheduling notes. More coverage is in the WWE category.
