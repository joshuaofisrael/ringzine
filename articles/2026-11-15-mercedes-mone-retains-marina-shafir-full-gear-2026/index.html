Mercedes Moné keeps the AEW Women's World title at Full Gear with her feet on the ropes

By RingZine

Moné survived Marina Shafir's Brutalizer and won with a cradle while using the ropes for leverage, leaving the Death Riders member with a clear claim to a rematch.

Last updated November 15, 2026

Mercedes Moné is still the AEW Women's World Champion, but only just. She beat Marina Shafir of the Death Riders at Full Gear in Phoenix on Saturday, and the way she did it leaves the door open for a rematch.

The finish

Moné tried Shafir's own Brutalizer submission, a nod to Shafir's friend PAC, F4W Online reports. Shafir reversed it and locked in a Brutalizer of her own. Moné shifted her weight into a cradle and put her feet on the ropes for leverage to get the pin, then left the area quickly to celebrate while Shafir stayed in the ring. AEW's results describe it as Moné just barely getting by in arguably the toughest match of her career.

The build

Shafir became the top contender after confronting Moné following her win over Thekla at WrestleDream, F4W says, and the match was made official at Fright Night Collision. On Wednesday, Shafir and Jon Moxley beat Moné and Jay White in a mixed tag match, with Moné tapping to the Brutalizer.

What comes next

Yahoo Sports says Shafir looked impressive in the biggest match of her career so far and has a clear path to a rematch after the champion used the ropes to get the win.
