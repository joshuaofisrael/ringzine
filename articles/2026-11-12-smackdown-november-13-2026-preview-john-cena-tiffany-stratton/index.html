WWE SmackDown preview, November 13, 2026: John Cena returns and Tiffany Stratton's first night as champion

By RingZine

WWE has announced John Cena for Friday's SmackDown in Fort Lauderdale to talk about the John Cena Classic, and Tiffany Stratton returns as the new WWE Women's Champion.

Last updated November 12, 2026

John Cena is coming back to SmackDown this Friday to talk about the John Cena Classic, WWE has announced. The show airs on Friday, 13 November, at 8 p.m. ET on USA, from the Amerant Bank Arena in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

Cena and the Classic

WWE says Cena has already visited Raw and NXT to invite wrestlers to the John Cena Classic, which takes place on Monday, 14 December, at Disney's ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex. He is now set to do the same on SmackDown, per WWE's preview. Last Word On Pro Wrestling lists Cody Rhodes, Zilla Fatu, Kelani Jordan, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno as wrestlers Cena has invited so far.

Stratton's first SmackDown as champion

Tiffany Stratton will also be on the show as the new WWE Women's Champion. WWE says she betrayed her friend Chelsea Green at Crown Jewel and cashed in her Money in the Bank contract for the second year in a row to take the title, in its announcement.

What else to expect

Those two segments are the only ones WWE has advertised so far. Last Word On Pro Wrestling expects Sami Zayn to address his Crown Jewel match with Roman Reigns, Chelsea Green to respond to losing the title, and Cody Rhodes to talk about Survivor Series, but WWE has not announced any of those.

WWE is also building towards Survivor Series: WarGames later this month, so more matches for that show could be added on Friday.
