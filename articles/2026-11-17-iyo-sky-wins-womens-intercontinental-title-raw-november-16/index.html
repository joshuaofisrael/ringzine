IYO SKY beats Raquel Rodriguez to win the Women's Intercontinental Championship on Raw

By RingZine

WWE announced the title change on X during Monday's Raw. The 946 also earned the advantage in the Men's WarGames Match, and LA Knight beat The Miz to join Team USA for Survivor Series.

Last updated November 17, 2026

IYO SKY is the new Women's Intercontinental Champion. WWE posted on X during Monday's Raw that "IYO SKY is the NEW Women's Intercontinental Champion", after she defeated Raquel Rodriguez.

How the title changed hands

According to WWE's Raw results, IYO SKY threw Rodriguez into Roxanne Perez while Becky Lynch dealt with Liv Morgan at ringside. SKY then hit the Over the Moonsault on Rodriguez for the win.

WarGames advantage to The 946

Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana of The 946 beat Jey and Jimmy Uso of The Bloodline to earn the advantage in the Men's WarGames Match at Survivor Series. WWE says Montana got his knees up to stop a Samoan Splash from Jey Uso, then hit Spin the Blood for the pin.

Team USA and the rest of the card

LA Knight beat The Miz to become the fourth member of Team USA in the Traditional Survivor Series Match. Afterwards CM Punk hit Kit Wilson with the GTS straight into a Cross Rhodes from Cody Rhodes. John Cena returned to explain The John Cena Classic and named Women's World Champion Liv Morgan as a participant, and Becky Lynch then confronted Morgan ahead of their title match at Survivor Series: WarGames.

Stephanie Vaquer's match with Jacy Jayne ended in a disqualification when Fatal Influence attacked Vaquer, and Bayley was taken out too when she tried to help.

What it means

The title change gives SKY gold heading into Survivor Series. Because The 946 hold the WarGames advantage, The Bloodline will be a man down for part of the Men's WarGames Match.
