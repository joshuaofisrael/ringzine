WWE Youngstown live event results, October 29, 2026: Gable beats Punk, Zayn retains

By RingZine

At WWE's Road to Survivor Series tour stop in Youngstown, Ohio, Chad Gable pinned CM Punk and Sami Zayn kept the Undisputed WWE Championship in a triple threat, according to Fightful and PWMania.

Last updated October 30, 2026

Chad Gable pinned CM Punk in a non title match and Sami Zayn retained the Undisputed WWE Championship over Cody Rhodes and Kevin Owens at WWE's Thursday live event in Youngstown, Ohio, Fightful reported. The show was part of the Road to Survivor Series: WarGames tour at the Covelli Centre.

WWE does not post results from untelevised live events, so this list comes from outlet reports, which is why it carries a rumor label. Fightful and PWMania, citing WrestlingBodySlam.com, published matching results.

Results

  • WWE United States Championship: Trick Williams (c) beat Damian Priest
  • Chelsea Green and Tiffany Stratton beat Nia Jax and Lash Legend. PWMania said Green pinned Jax.
  • Gunther beat Finn Bálor with a rollup, per PWMania.
  • WWE Women's Tag Team Championship: Michin and B Fab (c) beat Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley in a Halloween costume match. PWMania said Jade Cargill interfered.
  • Oba Femi, LA Knight and Solo Sikoa beat Bronson Reed, Bron Breakker and Austin Theory. PWMania said Femi pinned Theory.
  • Trick or Treat Street Fight: Danhausen beat Kit Wilson
  • Non title match: Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable beat CM Punk by pinfall
  • Undisputed WWE Championship, triple threat: Sami Zayn (c) beat Cody Rhodes and Kevin Owens. PWMania said Zayn pinned Owens.

Why it is worth noting

Zayn and Williams both kept their titles on Thursday, one night before they meet for the Undisputed WWE Championship on SmackDown. Our SmackDown preview has the full stakes.

Live event results do not change WWE's television storylines unless WWE references them on air.
