SmackDown results 13 November: women's WarGames teams set, Rey Fénix joins Team Mexico, Cena invites Charlotte

By RingZine

Charlotte Flair's team will face Fatal Influence, Jade Cargill and Michin inside WarGames at Survivor Series, John Cena added Charlotte and Fatal Influence to his Classic, and Gunther beat Finn Bálor.

Last updated November 14, 2026

Friday's SmackDown set the second WarGames match for Survivor Series and kept filling the John Cena Classic. "Odds evened. Teams set," Triple H posted on X after the show, and WWE confirmed the lineups on its Women's WarGames match page.

Women's WarGames is official

Charlotte Flair, Stephanie Vaquer, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley and Bayley will face Fatal Influence, Jade Cargill and Michin inside WarGames, WWE says. The match came out of Charlotte's bout with Women's United States Champion Jacy Jayne, which had no title on the line. According to the WWE.com results, it broke down into a brawl involving everyone on both sides, plus B Fab, before Charlotte's group laid down the WarGames challenge. Fights Around The World reports that Charlotte got the pinfall amid the chaos outside the ring.

Cena's Classic grows

John Cena returned to invite Charlotte to the John Cena Classic, which WWE marked with a welcome post on X. WWE also posted that Cena has invited Fatal Influence to join the Classic.

Rey Fénix joins Team Mexico

"The newest addition to Team Mexico is Rey Fénix," WWE announced on X. Fights Around The World describes the Survivor Series match as a traditional five on five elimination bout between Team Mexico and Team USA.

Other results and segments

  • Gunther def. Finn Bálor: WWE.com says Gunther pushed Bálor into the referee, landed a low blow while the official was dazed and finished him with the Powerbomb.
  • Tag Team Championship: Erik and Ivar of The War Raiders attacked during The MFTs' title defence against Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki and ended the match early, then assaulted both teams, WWE.com reports.
  • Damian Priest def. Axiom: Priest also hit Nathan Frazer with South of Heaven after the bell.
  • Kevin Owens and Sami Zayn: Owens confronted the Undisputed WWE Champion to demand a title match, and Johnny Gargano took a Stunner when he got between them.
  • Tiffany Stratton and Chelsea Green: the new WWE Women's Champion hit her former friend with a cheap shot, according to WWE.com.

Fights Around The World says Survivor Series: WarGames is two weeks away in Houston. Our Thursday preview has the earlier build.
