NXT Halloween Havoc 2026 card and how to watch on October 31

By RingZine

Grayson Waller defends the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo and Kelani Jordan faces Wren Sinclair at Halloween Havoc, live on The CW on Saturday, October 31.

Last updated October 28, 2026

NXT Halloween Havoc airs live on Saturday, October 31, at 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT on The CW and ESPN Unlimited, with Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo, WWE announced. Danhausen hosts the show.

Announced card

  • NXT Championship: Grayson Waller (c) vs. Tony D'Angelo
  • NXT Women's Championship: Kelani Jordan (c) vs. Wren Sinclair
  • Spin the Wheel, Make the Deal: Zilla Fatu vs. Charlie Dempsey
  • Weapons Wild Match: Tavion Heights vs. Myles Borne
  • Nontitle match: NXT Women's North American Champion Zaria vs. Izzi Dame
  • Singles match: Shiloh Hill vs. Naraku

The main storylines

Waller won the title from D'Angelo at Heatwave, and D'Angelo earned his rematch by beating Myles Borne on the October 13 NXT, per WWE's match page. The two brawled in a parking lot at the end of the October 27 episode, WWE reported.

In the storyline, Sinclair won a 20 woman battle royal while disguised as AAA's Lady Shani, and General Manager Robert Stone reinstated her and confirmed her as No. 1 contender, WWE said.

Heights and Borne are former tag team partners. Danhausen's wheel spin on the October 27 show landed on the Weapons Wild Match stipulation. Zaria offered Dame a title shot, but Dame turned it down and accepted a nontitle match instead.

WWE says more matches could be added before Saturday. RingZine will update this card if that happens.
