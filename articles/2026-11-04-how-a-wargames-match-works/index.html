How a WWE WarGames match ends: pinfall or submission once everyone is in

By RingZine

Analysis

A WWE WarGames match puts two rings inside one steel cage. Wrestlers enter on a clock, and a pinfall or a submission can end it only after everyone is inside. WWE printed that version for Survivor Series 2026.

Last updated November 21, 2026

A WWE WarGames match is won by pinfall or submission, and only after every wrestler is inside. WWE's Survivor Series: WarGames 2026 page says two rings sit inside one steel cage. One wrestler from each team starts. After five minutes, one more wrestler enters from one of the two teams. Every three minutes after that, another wrestler enters, and the teams take turns until the cage is full. There are no disqualifications, so a wrestler may bring a weapon in. The match itself starts when the last person is in. It then goes on until one team gets a pinfall or a submission. RingZine rechecked that page on 21 November 2026.

The clock WWE is using this month

WWE prints the same rules paragraph for both WarGames matches on the 2026 page. The first gap is five minutes. Every gap after that is three minutes. The page does not say which team sends the third person. It says one of the two teams does, and then the sides alternate.

That clock is not the original one. WWE's history of WarGames, published 18 September 2012, says the first two wrestlers fought for five minutes. A coin toss decided which team would be a wrestler short. That team waited two minutes before it could send someone in. The sides then alternated until everyone was inside. Only then, the history page says, did WarGames truly begin.

How the original finish differed

The 2012 history calls the early match The Match Beyond. The cage over the two rings had a roof. A team won when someone submitted or surrendered. The page does not list a pinfall as a way to win those early matches.

WWE dates the match to Jim Crockett Promotions in 1987. The first one, the history page says, was on 4 July 1987 at the Omni Coliseum in Atlanta, during the Great American Bash tour. Dusty Rhodes was in that match, on the team opposite the Four Horsemen. The page does not say he invented it.

Pinfalls came later. The same history page says the 1998 edition allowed them, and that Diamond Dallas Page pinned Stevie Ray.

What changed when WWE put the match on its own show

On 19 September 2022, WWE announced that Survivor Series would hold WarGames matches for men and for women. That was the first time the match was on a WWE branded event. The show was set for 26 November 2022 at TD Garden in Boston. The announcement said a steel cage would surround two rings, that one member of each team would start, and that more wrestlers would enter every few minutes. Once everyone was in, a pinfall or a submission could end it. It did not give the five minute and three minute clock that the 2026 page now prints.

The 2022 announcement and the 2026 page do not mention a roof. The roof is in the 2012 account of the original cage, not in the rules WWE is publishing for this month's show.

The matches set for 28 November

WWE's 2026 page says Survivor Series: WarGames is Saturday 28 November at Daikin Park in Houston. It streams at 6 p.m. ET and 3 p.m. PT on ESPN, on the ESPN App, with the Unlimited Plan. The page lists The Bloodline against The 946. That is the challenge Roman Reigns made on Raw, which RingZine covered in the Raw segment recap.

The same page lists Charlotte Flair, Stephanie Vaquer, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley and Bayley against Fatal Influence, Jade Cargill and Michin. How that match was made is in the 13 November SmackDown results.

A third match on the page is not WarGames. Women's World Champion Liv Morgan is booked to defend against Becky Lynch.

How the finish compares

AEW Blood and Guts also uses two rings under one cage. On the rules AEW announced in 2020, that match ends only when someone submits or surrenders. A Last Man Standing match is usually two people, and it ends when someone cannot beat a count of 10. WarGames, on the 2026 WWE page, needs the full field in the cage first, and then a pinfall or a submission. More WWE reporting is on the WWE desk.

Checked 21 November 2026 against WWE's Survivor Series: WarGames 2026 page, the 18 September 2012 history, and the 19 September 2022 announcement.
