Ethan Page Wins Intercontinental Title From Chad Gable on November 2 Raw

By RingZine

Ethan Page captured the Intercontinental Championship from Chad Gable during Monday Night Raw after a hard-fought title match in Brooklyn.

Last updated November 3, 2026

Ethan Page left the November 2 edition of WWE Raw as Intercontinental Champion, defeating Chad Gable in one of the programme's clearest in-ring changes. WWE's official results identify the title switch and describe how both wrestlers worked over a body part in the ring: Page went after Gable's neck and shoulder, while Gable focused on Page's knee. The finish came after Page used an exposed turnbuckle and followed with an Egoplex.

The sequence matters because the result was not presented as a straightforward exchange of signature moves. WWE says Gable used a Dragon Screw to compromise Page's leg and applied an Ankle Lock, while also delivering an Angle Slam onto the announce table. Those details show that the defending champion had a sustained route to victory. Page, however, was able to exploit Gable's neck issues at critical moments.

Gable also landed Chaos Theory, a move that would ordinarily give him an opportunity to secure a pin. According to WWE's account, the condition of his neck prevented him from maintaining the bridge needed to complete the cover. That created an opening for Page, who hit a Twisted Grin during the closing sequence before forcing Gable into the exposed corner. WWE records the subsequent Egoplex as the decisive move.

The result gives Page his first title on Raw, according to WWE. That is a narrower and more accurate description than suggesting it was his first championship anywhere in professional wrestling. The distinction is important in covering performers whose careers span different promotions and divisions. Page's new position on Raw is confirmed; any future challenger or rematch remains unannounced in the cited official results.

The title change occurred on a programme with several other major developments, including John Cena's announcement of the John Cena Classic and an escalating confrontation involving Roman Reigns, Sami Zayn and The 946. Those segments will compete for audience attention, but the Intercontinental Championship result is a concrete change to the weekly programme's competitive landscape. The belt has a new holder heading into the next broadcast.

There is also a notable difference between a match outcome and the storyline interpretation surrounding it. The exposed-turnbuckle finish gives Gable an obvious grievance within WWE's presentation, but that does not establish that a rematch has been booked. WWE's official page confirms the finish; it does not announce a return bout or disciplinary consequence for Page. Reporting either as certain would move beyond the available facts.

For viewers who missed Raw, the critical points are the limb-targeting match structure, Gable's unsuccessful attempt to capitalise on Chaos Theory, and Page's opportunistic finish. Those details explain how the title changed hands without inventing backstage reasons or attributing private intentions to the performers. They also distinguish this championship story from the separate faction and event announcements elsewhere on the show.

As of November 3, the verified development is Page's title win over Gable on November 2. WWE has not used the cited result to establish a long-term reign, a challenger list or the direction of the next Intercontinental Championship programme. Those questions remain open until the promotion makes further announcements.
