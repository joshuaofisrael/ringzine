WWE Raw holds at 2.5 million Netflix views for October 19 episode

By RingZine

Netflix's weekly Top 10 lists the October 19 Raw at 2.5 million views and 4.3 million hours viewed, ninth on its global shows chart.

Last updated October 28, 2026

The October 19 episode of WWE Raw drew 2.5 million views and 4.3 million hours viewed on Netflix, ranking ninth on the streamer's global Top 10 shows list for its week, according to Netflix's Top 10 chart. Netflix listed the episode's runtime as 1 hour 40 minutes.

How it compares

That matches the 2.5 million views for the October 12 episode and is up slightly from 2.4 million for October 5, per F4W's Raw viewership tracker. F4W's table shows Raw at 2.5 million views for most weeks since late September, with a high of 3.2 million for the September 14 episode.

The October 19 show featured Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn plus Penta vs. Bron Breakker, F4W noted.

How Netflix counts views

Netflix calculates views by dividing total hours viewed by runtime, so the figure is not the same as a traditional average audience. Numbers for the October 26 Raw are expected in next week's Top 10 release.
