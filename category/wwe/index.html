    Shawn Michaels expected to take Bruce Prichard's WWE creative role, report says

    PWInsider reports WWE plans to move Shawn Michaels into Bruce Prichard's creative writing and talent role after Prichard steps back following WrestleMania 43. WWE has not announced any change.

  • Confirmed WWE October 30, 2026

    WWE SmackDown preview, October 30, 2026: Zayn vs. Williams and Green vs. Bella

    Sami Zayn defends the Undisputed WWE Championship against Trick Williams and Chelsea Green defends the WWE Women's Championship against Nikki Bella on a Halloween themed SmackDown in Charleston, West Virginia.

  • Confirmed WWE October 30, 2026

    WWE stars join WhatsApp's The Chat: Bella Twins and LA Knight on November 1

    WWE and WhatsApp are running The Chat, a live football group chat on WhatsApp Channels. Nikki and Brie Bella and LA Knight are set for the Eagles vs. Commanders game on November 1.

  • Rumor WWE October 30, 2026

    WWE Youngstown live event results, October 29, 2026: Gable beats Punk, Zayn retains

    At WWE's Road to Survivor Series tour stop in Youngstown, Ohio, Chad Gable pinned CM Punk and Sami Zayn kept the Undisputed WWE Championship in a triple threat, according to Fightful and PWMania.

  • Confirmed WWE October 29, 2026

    How a WWE Last Man Standing match ends

    WWE's Last Man Standing match has no pinfall, no submission, and no disqualification. It ends when a wrestler cannot get up before a count of 10, and a draw leaves a title with the champion.

  • Confirmed WWE October 28, 2026

    NXT Halloween Havoc 2026 card and how to watch on October 31

    Grayson Waller defends the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo and Kelani Jordan faces Wren Sinclair at Halloween Havoc, live on The CW on Saturday, October 31.

  • Confirmed WWE October 28, 2026

    WWE Crown Jewel 2026 match card: four matches set for November 7 in Riyadh

    Roman Reigns vs. Sami Zayn and Liv Morgan vs. Chelsea Green headline Crown Jewel on November 7, but both SmackDown champions must first survive title defenses on October 30.

  • Confirmed WWE October 28, 2026

    WWE NXT results, October 27, 2026: Dusty Cup finals set, Drake retains

    Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno upset The Vanity Project and will meet Hank and Tank in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic final, while Jackson Drake kept the North American title.

  • Confirmed WWE October 28, 2026

    WWE Raw holds at 2.5 million Netflix views for October 19 episode

    Netflix's weekly Top 10 lists the October 19 Raw at 2.5 million views and 4.3 million hours viewed, ninth on its global shows chart.

  • Confirmed WWE October 27, 2026

    Penta and Je'Von Evans get a World Tag Team title match at Crown Jewel

    WWE made Penta and Je'Von Evans vs. Bron Breakker and Austin Theory for the World Tag Team Championship at Crown Jewel on November 7.

  • Confirmed WWE October 27, 2026

    WWE Raw results, October 26, 2026: Sami Zayn and Liv Morgan keep their titles

    Sami Zayn beat Je'Von Evans to keep the Undisputed WWE Championship, and Liv Morgan kept the Women's World Championship when Becky Lynch won by disqualification.
