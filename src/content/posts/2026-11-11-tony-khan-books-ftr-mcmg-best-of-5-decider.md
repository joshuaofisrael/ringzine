AEW president Tony Khan has booked the fifth and deciding match of a best-of-five series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns for Wednesday's combined Dynamite and Collision show.

In a [post on X](https://x.com/TonyKhan) on Tuesday evening, Khan wrote: "#AEWDynamite/#AEWCollision TOMORROW! Best of 5: Match 5 @AlexShelley313/@SuperChrisSabin vs Dax Harwood/@CashWheelerFTR In the 4th incredible battle, FTR have evened the score 2-2 vs MCMG in a classic last weekend! The deciding Match 5 is on Wednesday @AEWonTV live, TOMORROW!"

The Wednesday programme is a special three-hour Dynamite and Collision from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah, the go-home show before Saturday's Full Gear pay-per-view, [POST Wrestling reported](https://www.postwrestling.com/2026/11/09/tommaso-ciampa-vs-speedball-bailey-for-national-title-among-four-segments-set-for-wednesdays-three-hour-aew-dynamite/) on 9 November. That earlier report listed four segments already announced: Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher face to face; Mercedes Moné and Jay White versus Marina Shafir and Jon Moxley; Kofi Kingston and Austin Creed versus Bandido and Brody King; and Tommaso Ciampa defending the AEW National Championship against Speedball Mike Bailey. Khan's later post added the FTR-Machine Guns decider to that card.

AEW's own account also promoted the tag match between Kofi and Austin Creed and Bandido and Brody King, saying New Level "had no issue with Brodido until Bandido attacked Kofi" and that the teams will meet before their Full Gear Texas Death trios match.

Khan's post is the primary source for the 2-2 series score and for Match 5 being scheduled on Wednesday's live show. The earlier POST Wrestling preview did not yet include that bout. As with any live wrestling card, announced matches can still change before bell time.

This is a separate booking from Ciampa's first National title defence against Bailey, which this site has already covered from Khan's earlier announcement.
