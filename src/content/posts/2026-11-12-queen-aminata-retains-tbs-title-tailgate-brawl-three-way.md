Queen Aminata retained the TBS Championship on Wednesday's Collision, beating Skye Blue and Hyan in a three way match, [AEW said in its results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026). She pinned Blue after a high angle German suplex and her Off With Her Head kick.

## What happened after

AEW says Dr. Britt Baker distracted Aminata after the match so that Persephone could attack her from behind, before Maya World ran out to make the save. The company then confirmed that Aminata will defend the TBS title against Persephone and Maya World in another three way match on Saturday's Tailgate Brawl, [per the recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026). The [Wrestling Observer's report](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/aew-results/aew-dynamite-collision-live-results-november-11-2026/) lists the same match for the Tailgate Brawl show.

Persephone lost the TBS title to Aminata on Fright Night Dynamite two weeks ago, AEW notes. Earlier on Wednesday's show she beat Mac and spent time on commentary insisting the title was still hers.

Tailgate Brawl airs on Saturday before Full Gear in Phoenix.
