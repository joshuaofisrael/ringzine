MJF has picked his next fight, and it is for everything. "MJF Challenges Will Ospreay To Title vs. Title Match At AEW Worlds End," [Fightful posted on X](https://x.com/Fightful/status/2123271065447067951) after Wednesday's Dynamite.

## What happened on Dynamite

According to [Fightful's report](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling-news/339836-mjf-will-ospreay), MJF appeared on Dynamite days after his return at Full Gear and went after Ospreay, who was not on the show. He mocked the champion for not being there and picked apart Ospreay's "restore the feeling" line, claiming that he is the feeling instead.

With nobody interrupting, MJF claimed, in character, that Ospreay had taken his accolades, his title and, most importantly, his life. He ended the promo by challenging Ospreay to a match at Worlds End for both versions of the AEW World Championship.

AEW's own post from the segment, quoted by Fightful, said MJF "believes the AEW World Championship belongs to HIM, and he's ready to prove it at #AEWWorldsEnd!"

## Why there are two titles

MJF's return at Full Gear set this up. We covered how [Ospreay retained and MJF came back](/articles/2026-11-15-aew-full-gear-2026-ospreay-retains-mjf-returns/) that night. MJF is now presenting himself as holding his own version of the championship, which is why he is calling it title vs title.

## Is the match official?

Not yet. Fightful says it will update fans when, or if, the match is officially announced for Worlds End. Ospreay has not answered on television.

## What it means

Analysis: a promo with nobody answering is a challenge, not a booking. The next step is Ospreay's reply, and whether AEW puts both belts on the line at its year end show.
