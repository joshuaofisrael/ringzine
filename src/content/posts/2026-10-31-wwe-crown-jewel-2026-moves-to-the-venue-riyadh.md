**Crown Jewel is still on for Saturday, November 7, in Riyadh, but in a different building.** [PWInsider reported](https://www.pwinsider.com/article/213245/wwe-crown-jewels-new-venue-is.html?p=1) on Friday that the show has moved to The ANB Arena, also known as The Venue. It had been planned for Riyadh Season Stadium, an outdoor site.

## What the reports say

- **New building:** The Venue holds roughly 6,000 to 8,000 people depending on the production setup, according to PWInsider. The same arena hosts the Canelo Alvarez vs. Christian Mbilli boxing fight this weekend.
- **Tickets:** Earlier purchases were refunded and sales were paused, then reopened for the new venue, [F4W reported](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/wwe/wwe-crown-jewel-2026-tickets-back-on-sale/).
- **Why it moved:** F4W said one reason believed to be behind the switch is that an indoor arena offers added security amid regional security concerns.
- **Who announced it:** [eWrestlingNews](https://www.ewrestlingnews.com/news/wwe/riyadh-season-reveals-new-location-for-wwe-crown-jewel) reported that Riyadh Season announced the change and that the official ticketing page now lists the arena.

When RingZine checked on Saturday, [WWE's Crown Jewel event page](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwe-crown-jewel/crown-jewel-2026) still showed its original stadium announcement. RingZine has not seen a separate WWE statement on the move, so we are treating the venue as reported rather than confirmed by WWE.

## What has not changed

The date and broadcast are the same. Crown Jewel streams at noon ET and 9 a.m. PT on the ESPN App with an ESPN Unlimited plan in the United States and on Netflix internationally, WWE says. The four announced matches remain in place, and our [Crown Jewel match card](/articles/2026-10-28-wwe-crown-jewel-2026-match-card/) lists them, including Roman Reigns vs. Sami Zayn and Cody Rhodes vs. Randy Orton in a Last Man Standing match. F4W noted that a fifth match could still be added.

This story will be updated if WWE confirms the venue itself.
