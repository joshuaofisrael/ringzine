WWE said Kevin Owens defeated CM Punk in a No Holds Barred match on SmackDown.

The company [posted the finish](https://x.com/WWE) after promoting the bout live:

> "KO PUNCH.
>
> KEVIN OWENS BEATS CM PUNK IN NO HOLDS BARRED!!!"

Earlier in the same show window, WWE [announced](https://x.com/WWE) that Owens versus Punk was underway under No Holds Barred rules. Triple H amplified both the live posts and the result.

## Where the rivalry sits

WWE framed the match as the talking phase ending and the weapons coming out. The official result is Owens' win; WWE did not, in those posts, announce a rematch stipulation or a Survivor Series pairing tied directly to the finish. RingZine will watch follow-up promos for the next booked chapter.

Confirmed terms for now: No Holds Barred, SmackDown, Owens over Punk, per WWE's [result post](https://x.com/WWE).
