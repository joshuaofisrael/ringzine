**FTR beat the Motor City Machine Guns to tie their Best of 5 series at one win each, Jay White beat Kyle O'Reilly in the main event, and AEW confirmed a women's Blood and Guts match for November 4 in Jacksonville**, according to [AEW's official Collision results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-collision-results-october-24-2026). The show aired live from North Carolina.

## Full results

- **FTR def. the Motor City Machine Guns** in Match 2 of their Best of 5 series. Cash Wheeler sat down on an Alex Shelley victory roll while Stokely held Shelley's arm and Wheeler grabbed the rope, out of the referee's view, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-collision-results-october-24-2026). The series is tied 1 to 1.
- **Death Riders def. Shane Taylor Promotions** in a mixed tag match when Marina Shafir made Trish Adora submit.
- **Josh Alexander def. Blade Brown** by referee stoppage with an ankle lock.
- **Swerve Strickland def. Myron Reed** with the Swerve Stomp.
- **Mike Bailey and Andrade El Ídolo def. Steve Allen and Robert Allen.**
- **Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron def. Lena Kross and Megan Bayne by disqualification** after Julia Hart and Skye Blue interfered.
- **Jay White def. Kyle O'Reilly** with a second Blade Runner.

All results are from [AEW's recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-collision-results-october-24-2026).

## The Cut

White's alliance of the Bang Bang Gang and The Dogs is called The Cut in AEW's recap. White said the group wants Jon Moxley's AEW Continental Championship. After the main event, Cassidy accidentally hit Moxley with an Orange Punch when White ducked, and White hit Cassidy with the Blade Runner, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-collision-results-october-24-2026).

## Women's Blood and Guts

After Divine Dominion and Triangle of Madness attacked Wrathlander and Willow Nightingale, AEW confirmed Nightingale, Wrathlander, and The Brawling Birds against Triangle of Madness and Divine Dominion in Blood and Guts on November 4, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-collision-results-october-24-2026).

## Fright Night Dynamite card

AEW lists these matches for Fright Night Dynamite in St. Louis, [per the recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-collision-results-october-24-2026):

- TBS Championship: Persephone vs. Queen Aminata
- TNT Championship: Darby Allin vs. Josh Alexander
- Best of 5, Match 3: FTR vs. the Motor City Machine Guns
