**Ethan Page is the new Intercontinental Champion.** He beat Chad Gable on Monday's Raw at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, [WWE's official results confirm](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-11-02/results), and WWE chief content officer Triple H marked the win on X within minutes: "An Intercontinental Ego," his post read (linked in the sources below), congratulating "the new Intercontinental Champion."

## Page takes the Intercontinental Championship

Gable hit a Dragon Screw, the Ankle Lock and an Angle Slam onto the announce table, then landed Chaos Theory but could not hold the bridge for the pin, [according to WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-11-02/results). Page then sent him into an exposed turnbuckle and finished him with the Egoplex for his first title on Raw. RingZine previewed the match in the [Raw preview for November 2](/articles/2026-11-02-wwe-raw-november-2-2026-preview/).

## Cena reveals the John Cena Classic

John Cena, in his first Raw appearance since retiring, said the John Cena Classic will take place on December 14 at Disney World in Orlando, [Fightful reported](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/wwe-raw-results-11-2-2026-solo-sikoa-vs-royce-keys-john-cena-roman-reigns-more/), and he named Cody Rhodes as the first invited competitor. He also unveiled a new John Cena Classic Championship made for the event, and said Club WWE voting will play a role in the tournament, [SEScoops reported](https://www.sescoops.com/article/john-cena-unveils-john-cena-classic-championship). Randy Orton then interrupted to threaten Rhodes ahead of their Last Man Standing match.

## Full results

- Intercontinental Championship: Ethan Page def. Chad Gable (c) to win the title
- The Judgment Day (Liv Morgan, Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez) def. Chelsea Green, IYO SKY and Tiffany Stratton, with Morgan pinning Green
- The Vision (Bron Breakker, Bronson Reed and Austin Theory) def. Penta, Je'Von Evans and Dragon Lee
- Solo Sikoa vs. Royce Keys ended without a clean finish when The 946 attacked Sikoa. WWE lists it as a no contest, while [Fightful's results](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/wwe-raw-results-11-2-2026-solo-sikoa-vs-royce-keys-john-cena-roman-reigns-more/) record a disqualification win for Sikoa

## Reigns, Zayn and The 946

Roman Reigns opened the show and was interrupted by Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn, five days before they meet at Crown Jewel. The main event brawl ended with NXT's Cruz Montana taking out Reigns and siding with The 946, and Zayn closing the broadcast with a Helluva Kick to Reigns, [per WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-11-02/results).
