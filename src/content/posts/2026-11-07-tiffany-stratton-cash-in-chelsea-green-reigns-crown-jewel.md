Tiffany Stratton cashed in her Money in the Bank contract on her friend Chelsea Green to win the WWE Women's Championship at Crown Jewel in Riyadh on Saturday, minutes after Green had beaten Liv Morgan.

WWE [confirmed the title change on X](https://x.com/WWE/status/2119142091951628573):

> "After a successful cash-in on @ImChelseaGreen, @tiffstrattonwwe is your NEW WWE Women's Champion! 🏆"

## How the cash-in happened

Green, the WWE Women's Champion, beat Women's World Champion Liv Morgan to become Women's Crown Jewel Champion, reversing an Oblivion attempt into a rollup, according to [WWE.com's results](https://www.wwe.com/complete-crown-jewel-2026-results).

Morgan attacked Green during her post-match celebration, and Stratton ran out seemingly to help. After Green kept Judgment Day's Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez out of the ring, Stratton hit her with the briefcase, announced the cash-in and landed the Prettiest Moonsault Ever for the pin, WWE.com reported.

WWE's post of the turn, captioned "Some kind of friend you are", had passed 1.6 million views within hours.

## Reigns wins the men's Crown Jewel Championship

In the main event, World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns defeated Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn to become Men's Crown Jewel Champion, finishing him with back-to-back Spears. WWE [posted the result on X](https://x.com/WWE/status/2119155457931546682):

> ".@WWERomanReigns is your 2026 Crown Jewel Men's Champion! 🏆"

The finish followed a run of near falls and two referees being taken out, [CBS Sports reported](https://www.cbssports.com/wwe/news/wwe-crown-jewel-results-2026-live-updates-grades-winners/live/). Afterwards Reigns slapped away Zayn's offered handshake before the two embraced. WWE shared the moment with the quote: "You're not honorary. You're an Uso."
