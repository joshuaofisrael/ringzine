Team USA started Friday's SmackDown looking for a fifth member and ended it with fewer options. "Cody Rhodes To Unveil Team USA's Final Member On 11/23 WWE Raw," [Fightful posted on X](https://x.com/Fightful/status/2123977915343814868) after the show.

## Duggan backs out

According to [WWE's results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/smackdown/2026-11-20/results), Cody Rhodes and CM Punk spent the night trying to find a fifth member, with "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan and Trick Williams "entering the discussion". Duggan did not last long. Fightful's [show recap post](https://x.com/Fightful/status/2123977728248406282) describes him "appearing and backing out of being the 5th member of Team USA after seeing Team Mexico".

The same post says Rhodes ended up "getting a 5th team member", telling Punk: "How bad do you want to win?" Neither WWE nor Fightful has said who that is.

## Priest takes out Trick Williams

The other option went down in the ring. United States Champion Trick Williams faced Damian Priest in a non-title match, and "just as the match got going, Priest blasted Williams' knee with the ring bell", WWE says. Williams won by disqualification, but Priest kept attacking, "leaving Trick in serious pain and Team USA in dire straits".

WWE's video summary goes further, saying the attack is "taking Trick out of Survivor Series". WWE has not given an update on the knee itself, so treat that as the storyline for now.

## What happens next

Rhodes is due to name the final member on Monday's Raw, 23 November. With Duggan out and Williams hurt, the pick could be a surprise. That fits Rhodes's "How bad do you want to win?" line to Punk.
