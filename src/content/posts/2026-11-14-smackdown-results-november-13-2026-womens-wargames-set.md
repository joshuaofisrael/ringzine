Friday's SmackDown set the second WarGames match for Survivor Series and kept filling the John Cena Classic. "Odds evened. Teams set," Triple H [posted on X](https://x.com/TripleH/status/2121435179734741110) after the show, and WWE confirmed the lineups on its [Women's WarGames match page](https://www.wwe.com/shows/survivor-series-wargames/2026/womens-wargames-match).

## Women's WarGames is official

Charlotte Flair, Stephanie Vaquer, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley and Bayley will face Fatal Influence, Jade Cargill and Michin inside WarGames, [WWE says](https://www.wwe.com/shows/survivor-series-wargames/2026/womens-wargames-match). The match came out of Charlotte's bout with Women's United States Champion Jacy Jayne, which had no title on the line. According to the [WWE.com results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/smackdown/2026-11-13/results), it broke down into a brawl involving everyone on both sides, plus B Fab, before Charlotte's group laid down the WarGames challenge. [Fights Around The World](https://fightsatw.com/wwe-smackdown-11-13-results-womens-wargames-match-set-john-cena-delivers-more-invitations/) reports that Charlotte got the pinfall amid the chaos outside the ring.

## Cena's Classic grows

John Cena returned to invite Charlotte to the John Cena Classic, which WWE marked with a [welcome post on X](https://x.com/WWE/status/2121428784952062310). WWE also [posted](https://x.com/WWE/status/2121429052435378438) that Cena has invited Fatal Influence to join the Classic.

## Rey Fénix joins Team Mexico

"The newest addition to Team Mexico is Rey Fénix," WWE [announced on X](https://x.com/WWE/status/2121426499727249816). Fights Around The World describes the Survivor Series match as a traditional five on five elimination bout between Team Mexico and Team USA.

## Other results and segments

- **Gunther def. Finn Bálor:** WWE.com says Gunther pushed Bálor into the referee, landed a low blow while the official was dazed and finished him with the Powerbomb.
- **Tag Team Championship:** Erik and Ivar of The War Raiders attacked during The MFTs' title defence against Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki and ended the match early, then assaulted both teams, WWE.com reports.
- **Damian Priest def. Axiom:** Priest also hit Nathan Frazer with South of Heaven after the bell.
- **Kevin Owens and Sami Zayn:** Owens confronted the Undisputed WWE Champion to demand a title match, and Johnny Gargano took a Stunner when he got between them.
- **Tiffany Stratton and Chelsea Green:** the new WWE Women's Champion hit her former friend with a cheap shot, according to WWE.com.

Fights Around The World says Survivor Series: WarGames is two weeks away in Houston. Our [Thursday preview](/articles/2026-11-12-smackdown-november-13-2026-preview-john-cena-tiffany-stratton/) has the earlier build.
