Marina Shafir has a submission win over Mercedes Moné going into their AEW Women's World Championship match at Full Gear. Shafir and Jon Moxley beat Moné and Jay White in a mixed tag match on Wednesday's Dynamite when Shafir rolled through a pin attempt into the Brutalizer and Moné tapped, [AEW said](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026).

Everyone else was barred from ringside, so the rest of The Cut were sent to the back before the bell.

## The finish and the brawl

The [Wrestling Observer's report](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/aew-results/aew-dynamite-collision-live-results-november-11-2026/) says Moxley hit Moné with a cutter after she used a low blow to break up his choke on White, and that Moné tapped quickly while Moxley held White off. AEW's recap says Moné then attacked Shafir after the bell and The Cut ran in, before The Conglomeration came out to help the Death Riders. Moxley and Orange Cassidy shook hands at the end, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026).

## What it sets up

Moné defends the AEW Women's World Championship against Shafir at Full Gear on Saturday in Phoenix, [Yahoo Sports reports](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/article/aew-full-gear-2026-full-match-card-start-time-how-to-watch-will-ospreay-vs-kyle-fletcher-on-saturday-011152694.html). The Death Riders and The Conglomeration face The Cut on the same show in Anarchy in the Arena.

A win in a tag match does not change the title picture. Moné is still champion going into Saturday.
