Rey Fenix is next in line for the WWE Men's Intercontinental Championship after winning a triple threat over Chad Gable and Big Cass on Monday's Raw, [Sports Illustrated reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/wwe-raw-results-booking-grades-034647179.html).

## How the match came about

New champion Ethan Page, who beat Gable for the title on last week's Raw, opened his segment flanked by models and bragging about the win. Gable interrupted to congratulate him and ask for a rematch, which brought out Big Cass and then Fenix, each claiming the next shot. Page agreed they should fight for it, and general manager Adam Pearce made the number one contender triple threat official on the spot.

## The finish

Fenix broke up a pin with a dive off the top rope and used the opening to make his own cover for the win. [Stadium Rant](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/wwe-raw-results-november-9-082404507.html) also credited him with two Amigo Kicks on the way to the victory. Fenix is the current AAA World Cruiserweight Champion.

## The 946 crash the celebration

Fenix had barely started celebrating when The 946 stormed the ring and attacked all three men. Royce Keys then cut a promo warning that Roman Reigns' family would get the same treatment unless Reigns met him later and acknowledged him. That set up the main event segment, which ended with Reigns challenging The 946 to WarGames at Survivor Series.

## What comes next

No date has been announced for Page against Fenix. The attack leaves open whether The 946 will try to involve themselves in the title picture, and whether Gable, who lost the championship only a week ago, has another route back to a rematch.

For Fenix, the win adds a second singles title chase to his AAA championship and puts him at the front of what Raw appears to be building into a deeper Intercontinental division.
