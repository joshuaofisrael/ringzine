AEW's Halloween edition of Collision aired on Saturday, October 31, from the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, on TNT and HBO Max. The Triangle of Madness retained the Artist of Stardom Championships, Ilja Dragunov beat The Beast Mortos, and Tommaso Ciampa set up a title match for next week, according to [Fightful's results](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/aew-fight-night-collision-results-10-31-ilja-dragunov-competes/).

Here is what happened, match by match. For the build to the show, see our [Fright Night Collision preview](/articles/2026-10-31-aew-fright-night-collision-preview-october-31-2026/).

## AEW Collision results, October 31, 2026

- **Tommaso Ciampa def. Matt Sydal.** Ciampa will challenge Andrade El Idolo for the AEW National Title on next Saturday's Collision.
- **The Hurt Syndicate (Bobby Lashley and Shelton Benjamin, with MVP) def. Soloroto (Aaron Solo and Nick Comoroto, with Griff Garrison).**
- **Artist of Stardom Championships: The Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Julia Hart and Skye Blue) (c) def. Hazuki, Koguma and Fuwa-chan** to retain the titles.
- **Ilja Dragunov def. The Beast Mortos.** Ciampa stared Dragunov down from the top of the stage afterwards.
- **Mike Bailey def. Darius Martin.** Martin reluctantly shook Bailey's hand.
- **Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne and Lena Kross) def. Nixi Xs and Leela Feist.**
- **Konosuke Takeshita def. Adam Priest.**
- **The Cut (Austin Gunn, Colten Gunn and Juice Robinson) def. The Conglomeration (Orange Cassidy, Kyle O'Reilly and Roderick Strong).** The Cut attacked The Conglomeration after the match.

## Blood & Guts and other segments

- Persephone and Queen Aminata were added to the Blood & Guts match.
- Kris Statlander, Willow Nightingale, Harley Cameron and Queen Aminata talked about Blood & Guts, with Statlander noting the Brawling Birds were not there to help. The Triangle of Madness said they are not afraid to bleed in Blood & Guts.
- The Death Riders (Gabe Kidd, Jon Moxley, Marina Shafir, Wheeler Yuta and Daniel Garcia) addressed The Cut, with Garcia calling it Death Riders against the world.
- Samoa Joe said the perception of The Opps must change.
- Nick Wayne streamed himself celebrating his two wins over Chris Jericho.

## What's next

The confirmed match coming out of the show is Ciampa against Andrade El Idolo for the AEW National Title on next Saturday's Collision. We will post results the morning after.
