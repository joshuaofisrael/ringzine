Bayley will not be at tonight's NXT live event in Cleveland. In a social media post on Thursday, she told fans at the Agora Theatre she would not make the show because she has "some business to handle", [Fightful reported](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/bayley-says-she-wont-be-appearing-at-11-13-wwe-nxt-live-event-added-to-11-15-event/). The post included a SmackDown logo, which points to an appearance on Friday's SmackDown instead.

## Her weekend schedule

- **Friday 13 November, NXT Cleveland (Agora Theatre):** no longer appearing
- **Saturday 14 November, NXT Detroit:** still scheduled
- **Sunday 15 November, NXT Chicago:** newly added

The [Wrestling Observer](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/nxt/bayley-announces-changes-wwe-nxt-weekend-schedule-smackdown/) says the announcement came in an Instagram story and confirms the Detroit and Chicago dates. It adds that a Saturday match in Detroit would be her first in NXT since a three day tour in May.

## Why SmackDown

Fightful says Bayley has aligned with Charlotte Flair, Tatum Paxley and Alexa Bliss in their fight with Jade Cargill, B-Fab, Michin and Fatal Influence. WWE has not announced Bayley for tonight's show, so the logo is a hint rather than a booking. John Cena and Tiffany Stratton lead the advertised card; see our [SmackDown preview](/articles/2026-11-12-smackdown-november-13-2026-preview-john-cena-tiffany-stratton/).
