Ilja Dragunov's reign has its first challenger, and it came from saying no. "Dragunov declines to join the Don Callis Family. He will face El Clon on AEW Collision for the National Championship," [Fightful posted on X](https://x.com/Fightful/status/2123243696070271105) during Wednesday's Dynamite.

## What happened on Dynamite

Callis approached the new champion with an offer to join his group, [F4W reports](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/ilja-dragunov-national-title-defense-aew-collision/). Dragunov turned him down and instead offered to defend the title against any member of the family. Callis put forward El Clon, and the match was made official. AEW's own [Dynamite recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/news/was-don-callis-able-to-recruit-ilja-dragunov-into-the-family-aew-dynamite-111826) framed the segment around whether Callis could recruit the champion.

## Why it is his first defence

Dragunov only won the AEW National Championship on Saturday, beating Tommaso Ciampa at Full Gear, as we reported in our [Full Gear title story](/articles/2026-11-15-ilja-dragunov-wins-aew-national-title-full-gear-ciampa/). Saturday will be the first time he puts it on the line.

## The rest of Saturday's Collision

F4W lists four matches so far for the live show:

1. AEW National Champion Ilja Dragunov defends against El Clon
2. Continental Classic Blue League: Brody King vs Daniel Garcia
3. Continental Classic Blue League: Ricochet vs Jay White
4. Continental Classic Gold League: Tommaso Ciampa vs Kevin Knight

## What it means

Analysis: turning down Callis puts a target on Dragunov from the most crowded group in AEW. If El Clon falls short, expect Callis to keep sending names until one sticks.
