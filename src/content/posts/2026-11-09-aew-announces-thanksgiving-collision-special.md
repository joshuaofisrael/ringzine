AEW has announced a special Thanksgiving edition of Collision. It will be taped on Wednesday, November 25, after the Thanksgiving Eve edition of Dynamite, and will air the next night, [WrestleView reported](https://www.wrestleview.com/aew-news/397043-aew-announces-thanksgiving-edition-of-collision/). AEW announced the two-night holiday stretch on November 8.

## Schedule

| Show | Date | Time | Network |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dynamite: Thanksgiving Eve (live) | Wednesday, November 25 | 8 p.m. ET / 7 p.m. CT | TBS and HBO Max |
| Collision: Thanksgiving | Thursday, November 26 | 8 p.m. ET / 7 p.m. CT | TNT and HBO Max |

Both shows come from the LFCU Arena in Omaha, Nebraska. The only change for Collision is the night. It keeps its usual 8 p.m. Eastern start on TNT and HBO Max.

## The promo

AEW's announcement video is narrated by Tony Schiavone and features several AEW wrestlers. According to WrestleView, it also includes cameos from three wrestlers who are not under AEW contract: Scott Garland (formerly Scotty 2 Hotty), Curry Man and Glacier.

AEW described the stretch as "a double helping this Thanksgiving." WrestleView's report did not list any matches for either show.
