Tommaso Ciampa made his first successful defence of the AEW National Championship on Wednesday, beating Speedball Mike Bailey on the three hour Dynamite and Collision special, [AEW said in its results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026).

Ciampa won the title from Andrade El Ídolo on Collision last Saturday, and Bailey, like Andrade, is a member of United Empire.

## The match

AEW's recap describes a long, bloody match in which Bailey kicked out of several of Ciampa's big moves, including an avalanche Project Ciampa. Ciampa finally won with a running knee, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026). The [Wrestling Observer's report](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/aew-results/aew-dynamite-collision-live-results-november-11-2026/) says the crowd gave both men a standing ovation.

## Dragunov confirmed for Saturday

After the match a metronome appeared on the ramp and Ilja Dragunov came out. He pointed to the screens, which showed Ciampa versus Dragunov for the National title at Full Gear, [AEW said](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026). Both [AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026) and [Wrestleview](https://www.wrestleview.com/top-story/397168-aew-dynamite-collision-3-hour-special-11-11-26/) report that Ciampa offered a handshake, then made a rude gesture at Dragunov and left.

Full Gear is on Saturday, 14 November, in Phoenix. The National title match joins a card led by Will Ospreay against Kyle Fletcher for the AEW World Championship.
