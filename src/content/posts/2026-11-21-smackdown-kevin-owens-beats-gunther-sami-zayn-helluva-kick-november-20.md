Kevin Owens got his win over GUNTHER on Friday's SmackDown, but it was Sami Zayn who had the last word. "Sami Zayn lays out Kevin Owens to close the show," [Fightful posted on X](https://x.com/Fightful/status/2123975318818210237), asking: "When do you think KO is getting the title shot?" WWE's own [post](https://x.com/WWE/status/2123975818783142139) quoted the line "I didn't ask for any of this!" and said Zayn "leaves @FightOwensFight with a painful message".

## How the main event ended

According to [WWE's results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/smackdown/2026-11-20/results), GUNTHER blocked Owens's Swanton Bomb with his knees up and, "in an echo from last week", shoved Owens into the referee as he went for the Stunner. Candice LeRae and Johnny Gargano tried to distract Owens, but it backfired when he hit a Pop-Up Powerbomb on Gargano.

Then Finn Bálor got involved, kicking GUNTHER "below the belt". That "allowed KO to hit the Stunner, gaining the victory", WWE says. After the bell, "Sami Zayn blindsided Owens with a Helluva Kick".

WWE did not say on its results page whether the win earns Owens a title match. Fightful's question shows that is where fans expect this to go.

## The rest of the card

- **Jade Cargill def. Charlotte Flair** in a WarGames Advantage Match. Cargill pulled the padding off the second turnbuckle, shoved Flair into it, and got the pin.
- **The War Raiders def. Bakusai.** Haku's arrival distracted Kyoki, and The MFTs attacked both teams afterwards.
- **Trick Williams def. Damian Priest by disqualification** after Priest hit his knee with the ring bell. Our [Team USA story](/articles/2026-11-21-team-usa-fifth-member-trick-williams-knee-damian-priest-duggan-cody-rhodes-raw-reveal/) covers what that means for Survivor Series.
- **Rey Fenix def. Kit Wilson** in a non-title match with the Mexican Muscle Buster.
