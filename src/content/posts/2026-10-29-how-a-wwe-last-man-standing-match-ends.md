**A WWE Last Man Standing match does not end on a pinfall or a submission.** [WWE's history of the match](https://www.wwe.com/shows/vengeance/last-man-standing-history) says it continues, with no disqualifications, until one wrestler cannot get to their feet before a count of 10. RingZine rechecked that page on 20 November 2026.

## The rules WWE has published

[WWE's Extreme Rules 2013 page](https://www.wwe.com/shows/extremerules/2013/john-cena-ryback) states the stipulation directly. There are no disqualifications, count outs, pinfalls or submissions. The way to win is to leave an opponent unable to answer the referee's count of 10. The same page records the John Cena and Ryback match from that show as a no contest, because neither man got up. Both the rule and that result were still on the page when RingZine checked it on 20 November 2026.

[WWE's Extreme Rules 2011 results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/extremerules/2011/orton-punk) put the same rule in one sentence. Anything goes, there are no count outs, and the wrestler who does not answer the official's count of 10 loses. On that show Randy Orton won because CM Punk did not get up before the count. Rechecked 20 November 2026.

That finish is the whole match. A disqualification, which can end an ordinary WWE match, does not end this one.

## If neither wrestler gets up

WWE has ruled the match a draw when both wrestlers stay down through the count. [Its Backlash 2007 results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/backlash/2007/matches/396065217) say that in a draw, the championship leaves the same way it arrived, with the champion. On that show, neither Undertaker nor Batista answered the count, and Undertaker kept the World Heavyweight Championship. Rechecked 20 November 2026.

[WWE's history page](https://www.wwe.com/shows/vengeance/last-man-standing-history) describes the same kind of finish at Royal Rumble 2004. Shawn Michaels and Triple H were both still down at the count of 10, the match was a no contest, and Triple H kept the World Heavyweight Championship.

## The one WWE just ran

This is no longer a future booking. [WWE's Crown Jewel 2026 results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/crownjewel/2026/results), published 7 November 2026, say Cody Rhodes defeated Randy Orton in a Last Man Standing match. Rhodes punted Orton, and Orton stayed down for the count. RingZine's [recap of that match](/articles/2026-11-07-cody-rhodes-beats-randy-orton-lms-crown-jewel/) has the company's own posts from that night. The rest of the announced card is in the [Crown Jewel match card](/articles/2026-10-28-wwe-crown-jewel-2026-match-card/).

The finish is the same test as the older matches above. A pin or a submission would not have ended it. Orton had to beat the count, and WWE says he did not.

## How it differs from the cage matches

A [WarGames match](/articles/2026-11-04-how-a-wargames-match-works/) is a team match inside a cage, with staggered entries, and WWE's version can end on a pinfall. [AEW's Blood and Guts](/articles/2026-10-30-how-aew-blood-and-guts-match-works/) ends only when someone submits or surrenders. Last Man Standing is usually two people. The published rules do not require a cage. The referee's count of 10 is the whole finish. More WWE reporting is on the [WWE desk](/topics/wwe/).
