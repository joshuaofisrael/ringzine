Will Ospreay is still the AEW World Champion. He beat Kyle Fletcher in the main event of Full Gear at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix on Saturday night, and AEW [confirmed the result on X](https://x.com/AEW/status/2121849636533219662) just after the show. The bigger story came moments later, when MJF made his return.

## The main event

[Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/live/aew-full-gear-2026-live-results-updates-grades-analysis-tonight-for-will-ospreay-vs-kyle-fletcher-213000744.html) timed the match at 41 minutes. According to the [AEW results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-full-gear-results-november-14-2026), Fletcher kicked out of several of Ospreay's biggest moves before Ospreay hit consecutive Hidden Blades and finished with a Tiger Driver '91 for the pin. Yahoo Sports says the win seemingly closes the rivalry between the former friends, at least for now.

## MJF is back

As Ospreay celebrated with United Empire, Andrade El Ídolo raised his arm while holding up his contract for a world title match. Then a figure dressed in black attacked Ospreay from behind. AEW's results say the attacker revealed himself as MJF, stood on Ospreay's championship belt and held up his own title before leaving through the crowd. AEW [posted on X](https://x.com/AEW/status/2121849380873367709) that "MJF IS BACK!"

AEW later [said on X](https://x.com/AEW/status/2121868573115076882) that fans will hear from MJF on Dynamite this Wednesday, 18 November, in Denver, at 8pm Eastern on TBS and HBO Max, adding that he had held up his One True Championship.

## What it means

Yahoo Sports notes that Andrade still holds a guaranteed world title match, but suggests the way the show ended points to MJF as Ospreay's next challenger. The Continental Classic also begins on Wednesday's Dynamite, according to AEW.
