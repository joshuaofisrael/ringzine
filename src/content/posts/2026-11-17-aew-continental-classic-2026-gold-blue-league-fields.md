AEW has named the twelve wrestlers in this year's Continental Classic. The [official tournament page](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/continental-classic) now lists both leagues, after the field was revealed during a selection special on Monday night.

## Gold League

Jon Moxley, Kevin Knight, Orange Cassidy, Nick Wayne, Speedball Bailey and Tommaso Ciampa.

## Blue League

Jay White, Hangman Adam Page, Brody King, Daniel Garcia, Ricochet and Bandido.

## How the tournament works

Each wrestler faces the other five in their league. A win is worth three points and a draw one point, with ties broken by head to head record, AEW says. Matches are under Continental Rules: a 20 minute time limit, nobody allowed at ringside and a strict ban on outside interference, under threat of being banned from AEW.

The top two in each league advance. The winner of the Blue League meets the runner up of the Gold League in the semifinals, and the other way round. Both semifinals and the final take place at AEW Worlds End in Minneapolis on 19 December. Before that, the league matches run across episodes of Dynamite and Collision, with stops listed in Omaha, San Antonio and Oklahoma City.

## Who to watch

Moxley and Page are former AEW World Champions, and the Gold League mixes veterans like Ciampa with younger names in Knight, Wayne and Bailey. Every wrestler starts at zero points, so early results on Dynamite and Collision will quickly shape both tables.
