**Tony Khan booked AEW World Champion Will Ospreay against Kyle Fletcher at Full Gear, and AEW posted four matches for Fright Night Collision on Saturday.** Both came out of Fright Night Dynamite, [AEW's results page said](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026). Who won the title matches that night is in the [Dynamite results](/articles/2026-10-29-aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026/).

## Full Gear

Fletcher beat Jungle Jack Perry on Dynamite, then said he would challenge Ospreay. AEW wrote that Tony Khan made Ospreay versus Fletcher official for the world title at Full Gear. [AEW's Fright Night preview](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-preview-october-28-2026) dated Full Gear as November 14 in Phoenix.

Jon Moxley, after The Cut beat him and Daniel Garcia, told The Cut he wants Anarchy in the Arena at Full Gear, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026). That is Moxley calling for the match. The results page does not say it has been added to the card.

Swerve Strickland appeared on a video asking again for a Texas Death match at Full Gear, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026). AEW did not say that match was made official.

## Blood and Guts

The same preview dated Blood and Guts as November 4 in Jacksonville. On Dynamite, AEW said a women's Blood and Guts match is set: Triangle of Madness and Divine Dominion against the AEW Women's World Tag Team Champions, the Brawling Birds, with Wrathlander and Willow Nightingale, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026).

Steven Borden said on the show that he will team with the Young Bucks in the men's match, and that he wants to be there, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026).

## Fright Night Collision

Collision is Saturday from Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, on TNT and HBO Max at 8 p.m. ET and 7 p.m. CT, [AEW said](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026). That Saturday is October 31.

Announced matches:

- The Conglomeration vs. The Cut, with Juice Robinson and the Gunns named for The Cut
- Ilja Dragunov vs. The Beast Mortos
- Tommaso Ciampa vs. Matt Sydal
- Artist of STARDOM Championship: Triangle of Madness defend in a trios match. AEW's results page names the challengers, including Hazuki and Koguma.

Roderick Strong had challenged any three members of The Cut for Collision. AEW wrote that Tony Khan then made the Conglomeration trios match official.
