Kelani Jordan is still NXT Women's Champion, but she did not beat Kendal Grey to stay there. According to [WWE's NXT results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/2026-11-17/results) for Tuesday 17 November, Grey won their title match by disqualification after Jordan slapped the referee, so the championship stayed with Jordan.

## How the match went

WWE says this was Grey's first chance to win back the title from Jordan, the same wrestler who took it from her, after months out of action. Grey hit a facebuster that got only a two count, and Jordan's 450 splash did not finish Grey either.

Frustrated, Jordan removed part of the second turnbuckle padding. Grey kept going back to the Ankle Lock, and Jordan kept fighting her way out. Grey then hit a leaping paydirt from the top rope, and Jordan kicked out again.

When Grey locked in the Ankle Lock once more, Jordan slapped the referee while still in the hold. That ended the match in a disqualification win for Grey, and Jordan walked away with her title.

## Elsewhere on the show

Kam Hendrix beat Tony D'Angelo after Myles Borne distracted D'Angelo, and Chantel Monroe beat Layla Diggs. Tristan Angels won a triple threat match against Sean Legacy and Saquon Shugars with a roll up. Hank, Tank, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno said they will face all of The Vanity Project, including Myka Lockwood, in an eight person tag team match next week.

## What it means

A disqualification finish keeps the story open. Grey won the match but not the belt, and Jordan showed she was willing to lose on purpose to keep it. WWE has not announced a rematch.
