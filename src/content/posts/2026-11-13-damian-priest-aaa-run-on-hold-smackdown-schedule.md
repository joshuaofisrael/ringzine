Damian Priest has explained why he disappeared from AAA after a summer debut. Speaking on the Complex Graps podcast, the SmackDown star said the run is on hold because of his WWE schedule, [Fightful reported](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/338699-damian-priest-aaa/).

## What Priest said

Priest told the podcast that travelling to Mexico for Saturday shows after working SmackDown on Fridays was the problem. He said he was advertised for two AAA shows and missed both, and that he felt bad because it made him look like he was skipping dates and made the brand look bad. According to [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/fannation/wrestling/wwe/damian-priest-reveals-why-his-aaa-run-came-to-an-abrupt-end), he said the two sides then agreed to pause the run, with the idea that he could spend a few months with AAA if he ever gets a break from SmackDown, something he said he has never had.

He also said there was a lot he still wanted to do there, "including winning that championship."

## His AAA run so far

[Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/fannation/wrestling/wwe/damian-priest-reveals-why-his-aaa-run-came-to-an-abrupt-end) reports that Priest debuted for AAA on the 4 July show, when Rey Mysterio introduced him as a key signing, and cut a promo in Spanish saying he was coming for the AAA champions. His only other appearance was a four way match for the vacant AAA Latin American Championship against La Parka, El Fiscal and El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. at Ola De Calor, which ran alongside NXT Heatwave.

[Wrestling Headlines](https://wrestlingheadlines.com/damian-priest-addresses-aaa-no-shows-reveals-talks-with-wwe-about-his-future/) notes that Priest also pointed to the Undertaker's involvement in AAA as a reason he would like to go back.

## Where he is now

On SmackDown, Priest wrestled Nathan Frazer on the 6 November episode, Fightful says. There is no date for a return to AAA.
