All Elite Wrestling will present a special three-hour episode of Dynamite and Collision tonight, 11 November, live at 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. CT) on TBS and HBO Max. In its [official preview](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-preview-november-11-2026), AEW said the show comes from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah, and is the last television stop before Full Gear on Saturday in Phoenix, Arizona.

The card is built around matches that point to the pay-per-view. AEW Women's World Champion Mercedes Moné and Jay White face AEW Continental Champion Jon Moxley and Marina Shafir in a mixed tag that the promotion billed as a preview of Moné's title defense against Shafir and of Anarchy in the Arena, where the Death Riders meet The Cut. AEW World Champion Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher will also come face to face three days before their World Championship match at Full Gear.

Two-thirds of the AEW World Trios Champions, Brodido (Bandido and Brody King), face The New Level (Austin Creed and Kofi) ahead of the Trios Championship Texas Death match at Full Gear, where "Hangman" Adam Page and Swerve Strickland also become involved, the preview says. Tommaso Ciampa is scheduled to make his first defence of the AEW National Championship against "Speedball" Mike Bailey. The Best of 5 series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns, tied 2-2, is also listed for the deciding fifth match.

The start time, venue and broadcast details are as published by AEW on 11 November. Lineups can still change before bell time.
