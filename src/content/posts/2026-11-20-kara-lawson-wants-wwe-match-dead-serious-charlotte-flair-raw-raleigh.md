One of the best known coaches in basketball wants a spot on a WWE card, and she says it is not a joke. "Kara Lawson wants to wrestle in WWE!" WRAL sportscaster [Chris Lea posted on X](https://x.com/ChrisLeaTV/status/2123528885832909263) on Thursday, after asking the Duke coach about her night at Monday Night Raw in Raleigh.

## Front row, thanks to Charlotte Flair

Lawson, head coach of Duke women's basketball and of the Team USA women's team, sat at ringside at the Lenovo Center for the 16 November Raw. Lea's post says Charlotte Flair gave her the tickets.

[Fightful transcribed her answer](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling-news/duke-team-usa-womens-basketball-head-coach-kara-lawson-reflects-on-experience-at-wwe-raw-says-shes-dead-serious-about-competing-in-a-match), given after Duke's 111 to 44 win over Fordham on Thursday. "She (Charlotte Flair) gave me tickets to Raw," Lawson said, and she listed the names she saw: Cody Rhodes, The Miz, John Cena, Oba Femi, and the tag team match with The Usos.

The brawl came right to her. "We were right in the action. They came fighting right near us. I had to sit down. I thought they were coming in. They threw in the table in front of us."

## "Somebody tell Triple H"

Then came the pitch. "Hopefully sometime I'll get to be in a match. That's my goal. I know y'all are laughing. I'm dead serious," Lawson said, according to Fightful. "If they have a draft or something, they need to draft me. I will come in and I will wreck some stuff in that ring."

She even has a to do list: "I have to think of a name, what my name's gonna be, but somebody tell Triple H and Stephanie McMahon I'm ready."

## Will it happen?

WWE has not responded publicly, and nothing is booked. Celebrity appearances are a regular part of WWE shows, but a sitting college and national team head coach stepping into a match would be unusual, so for now this is a fan's wish rather than a plan. Lawson did promise one thing: "Any time they come, I'm going."

Raw continues on Mondays on Netflix, and Friday's SmackDown is in Evansville, Indiana, Fightful notes.
