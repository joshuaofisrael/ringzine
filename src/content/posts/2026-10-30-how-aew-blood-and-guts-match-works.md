**An AEW Blood and Guts match is won only by submission or surrender.** When AEW announced the rules on Dynamite in March 2020, [Cageside Seats reported](https://www.cagesideseats.com/2020/3/11/21175810/aew-dynamite-mar-11-2020-blood-guts-rules-wargames-inner-circle-elite) that the winning team is decided that way. The report does not list a pinfall as a way to win. RingZine rechecked it on 20 November 2026.

## The setup

The match uses two rings covered by one giant cage, the same Cageside report said. Wrestlers can move between both rings inside the structure. That is the part it shares with [WWE's WarGames match](/articles/2026-11-04-how-a-wargames-match-works/), which also puts two rings under one cage. WWE's version can end on a pinfall. This one cannot, on the rules AEW announced in 2020.

## How wrestlers enter

One wrestler from each team starts inside the cage for a five minute opening. After that, another participant enters every two minutes. In the 2020 version, the side that gained the entry advantage was settled by an earlier six man tag on Dynamite.

AEW said the match officially begins only once every competitor is inside the cage. Until then, nobody can win. Those entry rules were still the ones in the Cageside report when RingZine checked it on 20 November 2026.

## The one AEW has already run

The Don Callis Family accepted a men's Blood and Guts match for November 4, 2026 in Jacksonville, [according to AEW's October 21 Dynamite results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026). The booking from that night is in RingZine's [Dynamite recap](/articles/2026-10-22-aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026/).

That date has passed. The November 4 Dynamite aired, and RingZine's [ratings story](/articles/2026-11-07-aew-blood-and-guts-beats-nxt-ratings/) covers what Tony Khan and F4W/WON reported about the television audience. This page does not say who submitted in the cage. RingZine has not published a match result for those bouts, and this explainer will not guess one.

## How the finish compares

[A WWE Last Man Standing match](/articles/2026-10-29-how-a-wwe-last-man-standing-match-ends/) is a different test. One person fails a referee's count of 10, and the published rules do not require a cage. Blood and Guts needs the submission or the surrender, from the 2020 announcement, after everyone is inside.
