**An AEW Blood and Guts match has no pinfalls and no count outs.** When AEW first announced the rules on Dynamite in March 2020, it said a team wins only by making any opponent submit or surrender, [as reported by WrestleZone](https://www.wrestlezone.com/news/1176804-aew-blood-and-guts-match-rules-announced).

## The setup

The match uses two rings covered by one giant cage with a roof, [Cageside Seats reported](https://www.cagesideseats.com/2020/3/11/21175810/aew-dynamite-mar-11-2020-blood-guts-rules-wargames-inner-circle-elite) from AEW's announcement. Wrestlers can move between both rings inside the structure.

## How wrestlers enter

One wrestler from each team starts inside the cage for a five minute opening round. After that, another participant enters every two minutes, [per Wrestling Attitude](https://www.wrestlingattitude.com/2020/03/aew-explains-blood-and-guts-match-rules.html). In the first version, which side gained the entry advantage was settled by an earlier six man tag match on Dynamite.

AEW said the match officially begins only once every competitor is inside the cage. Until then, nobody can win.

## How it ends

The only ways to win are a submission or a surrender. Escaping the cage does not count, and neither does a pinfall, [WrestleZone reported](https://www.wrestlezone.com/news/1176804-aew-blood-and-guts-match-rules-announced). That is why these matches often run long.
