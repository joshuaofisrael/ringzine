**AEW closes its Fright Night week with a Halloween Collision from Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis.** The show airs Saturday, October 31, at 8 p.m. ET and 7 p.m. CT on TNT and HBO Max, [AEW announced](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-collision-preview-october-31-2026). [Pro Wrestling Dot Net notes](https://prowrestling.net/site/2026/10/31/aew-collision-lineup-tonights-fright-night-edition/) that the episode was taped on Wednesday, so this preview avoids spoilers.

## Announced card

- **Trios:** The Cut (Juice Robinson, Austin Gunn and Colten Gunn) vs. The Conglomeration (Orange Cassidy, Kyle O'Reilly and Roderick Strong)
- **Artist of STARDOM Championship:** Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Skye Blue and Julia Hart) (c) vs. the Trinity trio led by Hazuki and Koguma
- Ilja Dragunov vs. The Beast Mortos
- Tommaso Ciampa vs. Matt Sydal
- Konosuke Takeshita vs. Adam Priest
- "Speedball" Mike Bailey vs. Darius Martin
- Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne and Lena Kross) in action
- The Hurt Syndicate (Bobby Lashley and Shelton Benjamin, with MVP) in action

## The Cut vs. The Conglomeration

This one grew out of Jay White's turn on Orange Cassidy on Dynamite just over a week ago, AEW said. White then unveiled his new group on Collision and beat O'Reilly. On Fright Night Dynamite, Strong challenged any three members of The Cut, and the same night The Cut beat the Death Riders and laid out Claudio Castagnoli. Our [Fright Night Dynamite results](/articles/2026-10-29-aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026/) have the full story.

## A rare STARDOM title defense

AEW calls the Artist of STARDOM defense a rare appearance of a STARDOM championship on its shows. Trinity earned the shot after a win over Hart in Japan last month, according to the promotion. Triangle of Madness also has Women's Blood and Guts on Wednesday in Jacksonville, where they team with Divine Dominion against the Brawling Birds and Wrathlander. Our explainer covers [how Blood and Guts works](/articles/2026-10-30-how-aew-blood-and-guts-match-works/).

## Dragunov and Ciampa on the same show

Dragunov and Ciampa came face to face on Dynamite, and Ciampa turned down a fight on the spot, AEW said. Neither faces the other tonight. Dragunov makes his Collision debut against Mortos, while Ciampa meets Sydal in Sydal's hometown.

RingZine will post Collision results after the broadcast.
