**Nikki Bella, Brie Bella and LA Knight will give live reactions to the Philadelphia Eagles vs. Washington Commanders game on Sunday, November 1, through WhatsApp's The Chat,** [WWE announced](https://www.wwe.com/article/whatsapp-and-wwe-join-forces-to-bring-the-football-group-chat-to-life). The Bellas are longtime Eagles fans, and Knight will root for Washington. WWE said more talent will be named closer to the game.

## What The Chat is

The Chat is a live group conversation run by WhatsApp and OffBall that lets fans follow athletes and celebrities reacting to games in real time. This season it moved to WhatsApp Channels, which broadcast one way to an unlimited number of followers, according to WWE's announcement. Fans can read along but the posts come from the featured guests.

WhatsApp and OffBall created the format in 2025. Earlier editions featured LeBron James, Deion Sanders, Daniel Ricciardo and Kylie Kelce, WWE said.

## How the WWE run started

The WWE edition launched on October 18 with the Dallas Cowboys vs. Green Bay Packers game. Cody Rhodes and Trick Williams represented Dallas, Liv Morgan backed Green Bay, and The Miz added play by play commentary.

WWE gave a reason for each pick. Rhodes' sister is a former Cowboys cheerleader, and Williams spent time in the NFL before WWE.

## Why it matters

The partnership puts WWE talent in front of a football audience on a messaging app WWE describes as used by more than 3 billion people. It also lands during a busy stretch on the wrestling calendar, with [Crown Jewel on November 7](/articles/2026-10-28-wwe-crown-jewel-2026-match-card/).

WWE has not said how many games the WWE version will cover beyond November 1, only that The Chat continues through the season.
