Zilla Fatu has shown fans a package John Cena sent him ahead of the first John Cena Classic, [PWMania reported](https://www.pwmania.com/john-cena-sends-zilla-fatu-special-package-ahead-of-john-cena-classic). It included two gold challenge coins whose role in the tournament Fatu is keeping secret for now.

## What was in the box

According to PWMania, the package from the 17-time world champion contained:

- A John Cena-branded jacket
- Two gold challenge coins
- A deck of John Cena Classic playing cards
- A "Rise Above Hate" wristwatch

Fatu said the watch was the first he had ever owned and that he had been complaining about needing one that very morning. "Oh my gosh, I love this. That's my life, man. Rise above hate," he said. "And it's a John Cena Classic watch. The first ever."

Mason Rook appeared in the video and joked about whether Fatu could tell time. "I can actually read it, yes sir," Fatu replied.

## The challenge coins

Fatu said Cena included a letter explaining how the two gold coins will be used during the John Cena Classic. He did not reveal the instructions, so their purpose is still unknown.

## About the John Cena Classic

- **Date:** Monday, December 14
- **Venue:** ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex at Walt Disney World, Orlando
- **Format:** an interbrand tournament with talent from Raw, SmackDown and NXT. PWMania says Club WWE members will help pick the participants and decide the winner.
- **Prize:** the John Cena Classic Championship, a belt inspired by Cena's spinner title.
- **Confirmed entrants:** Cody Rhodes was announced first. Fatu, introduced as a participant on the November 4 episode of NXT when Cena appeared at the WWE Performance Center, is the second.
