The first Monday Night Raw after Crown Jewel takes place on 9 November at Bon Secours Wellness Arena in Greenville, South Carolina, with a 7:30 p.m. local start, according to [WWE's event page](https://www.wwe.com/event/monday-night-raw-73).

## Who is advertised

The event page lists five featured superstars: Roman Reigns, Oba Femi, Liv Morgan, Dominik Mysterio and Sol Ruca. Advertised names are a guide to who is booked to appear, not a match card, and WWE had not announced any matches or segments for the show when this preview was published.

## Crown Jewel fallout

Reigns arrives on Raw after beating Sami Zayn to win the men's Crown Jewel Championship, as RingZine [reported](/articles/2026-11-07-tiffany-stratton-cash-in-chelsea-green-reigns-crown-jewel/). How WWE follows up on that result, and on the rest of the Crown Jewel card, is the main question going into Monday.
