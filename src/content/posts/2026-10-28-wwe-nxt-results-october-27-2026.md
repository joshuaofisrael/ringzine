**Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno upset NXT Tag Team Champions The Vanity Project, and Hank and Tank beat the AAA World Tag Team Champions, setting the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic final on the October 27 NXT.** Jackson Drake also retained the NXT North American Championship against EK Prosper, [according to WWE's official results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/2026-10-27). This was the final NXT before Halloween Havoc on Saturday, October 31.

## Full results

- **Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno def. The Vanity Project** in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic semifinal. Dar and Moreno avoided interference from Myka Lockwood and finished with a High Low combination, [per WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/2026-10-27).
- **Chantel Monroe def. Lizzy Rain** in Monroe's NXT singles debut. WWE called the finish controversial, saying Monroe grabbed the ropes while pinning Rain.
- **Sean Legacy def. Saquon Shugars** after countering a splash into a rollup.
- **Hank Walker and Tank Ledger def. El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno** in the other semifinal, advancing to the tournament final.
- **NXT North American Champion Jackson Drake def. EK Prosper.** WWE said Kam Hendrix knocked Prosper off the top rope while Lockwood distracted the referee, leading to the win for Drake.

## What it sets up

Host Danhausen announced that Tavion Heights and Myles Borne will meet in a Weapons Wild Match at Halloween Havoc. The show ended with NXT Champion Grayson Waller and Tony D'Angelo fighting in a parking lot four days before their title match, [WWE reported](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/2026-10-27).

The Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic final pits Dar and Moreno against Hank and Tank. WWE had not announced a date for the final in its results.
