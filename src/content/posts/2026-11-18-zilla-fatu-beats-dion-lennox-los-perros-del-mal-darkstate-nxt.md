Zilla Fatu picked up a win over Dion Lennox of DarkState on Tuesday's NXT, with help from an unexpected source. [WWE's results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/2026-11-17/results) say Los Perros del Mal arrived in the middle of the match to brawl with DarkState, and Fatu used the chaos to finish Lennox.

## How it happened

Fatu went for a hip attack on Lennox against the ring steps, but Lennox moved and Fatu crashed into the steel. Lennox thought he had the match won, WWE says, but Fatu kept fighting while the rest of DarkState cleared the announce table at ringside.

Just as the match was heating up, Los Perros del Mal came out and started fighting DarkState. Fatu took advantage and hit a big frog splash for the win.

## Why Los Perros del Mal matter here

Los Perros del Mal attacking DarkState sets up a clear rivalry between the two groups. WWE's recap does not say why they got involved.

## The rest of the card

In the main title story of the night, Kendal Grey beat NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan by disqualification, so Jordan kept the belt. NXT Women's North American Champion Zaria also confronted Izzi Dame with a verbal tirade, and Kam Hendrix beat Tony D'Angelo after interference from Myles Borne.
