The brothers known in WWE as Julius and Brutus Creed are now signed to AEW. Wrestling as Jacob and Drew Kasper, they debuted at Full Gear in Phoenix on Saturday, and Tony Khan then posted that "the Kasper Brothers are ALL ELITE," [Fightful reports](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/the-kasper-brothers-sign-with-all-elite-wrestling/).

## The debut

The pair arrived after The Young Bucks retained the AEW World Tag Team Championship, and went after both the champions and the number one contenders, the Motor City Machine Guns, according to [Wrestling Headlines](https://wrestlingheadlines.com/tony-khan-comments-on-signing-the-creed-brothers-just-one-week-after-their-wwe-releases/). FTR then came out to face them, and Stokely Hathaway calmed things down. Hathaway worked with the brothers in WWE NXT as part of the Diamond Mine group.

[Wrestling Inc.](https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2317027/aew-full-gear-2026-kasper-brothers-creeds-wwe-debut-tag-titles-young-bucks/) reports that the brothers left WWE after their contracts expired and were not renewed. Fightful says they were moved to the alumni section of WWE's website, and that their last WWE match came in October in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, a tournament they won in 2022. They also held the NXT Tag Team Championship.

## What we do not know

Khan's post confirms the signing but gives no contract length or terms. AEW has not said when the Kasper Brothers will wrestle next, or whether they are aligned with FTR. Dynamite is on Wednesday, 18 November, in Denver.
