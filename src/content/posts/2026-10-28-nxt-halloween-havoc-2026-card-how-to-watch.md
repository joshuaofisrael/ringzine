**NXT Halloween Havoc airs live on Saturday, October 31, at 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT on The CW and ESPN Unlimited, with Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo**, [WWE announced](https://www.wwe.com/shows/nxtple/nxt-halloween-havoc-2026). Danhausen hosts the show.

## Announced card

- **NXT Championship:** Grayson Waller (c) vs. Tony D'Angelo
- **NXT Women's Championship:** Kelani Jordan (c) vs. Wren Sinclair
- **Spin the Wheel, Make the Deal:** Zilla Fatu vs. Charlie Dempsey
- **Weapons Wild Match:** Tavion Heights vs. Myles Borne
- **Nontitle match:** NXT Women's North American Champion Zaria vs. Izzi Dame
- **Singles match:** Shiloh Hill vs. Naraku

## The main storylines

Waller won the title from D'Angelo at Heatwave, and D'Angelo earned his rematch by beating Myles Borne on the October 13 NXT, [per WWE's match page](https://www.wwe.com/shows/nxtple/nxt-halloween-havoc-2026/nxt-title). The two brawled in a parking lot at the end of the October 27 episode, [WWE reported](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/2026-10-27).

In the storyline, Sinclair won a 20 woman battle royal while disguised as AAA's Lady Shani, and General Manager Robert Stone reinstated her and confirmed her as No. 1 contender, [WWE said](https://www.wwe.com/shows/nxtple/nxt-halloween-havoc-2026).

Heights and Borne are former tag team partners. Danhausen's wheel spin on the October 27 show landed on the Weapons Wild Match stipulation. Zaria offered Dame a title shot, but Dame turned it down and accepted a nontitle match instead.

WWE says more matches could be added before Saturday. RingZine will update this card if that happens.
