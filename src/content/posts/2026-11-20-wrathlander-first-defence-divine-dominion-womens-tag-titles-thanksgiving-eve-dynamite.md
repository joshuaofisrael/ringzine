The new champions did not get long to celebrate. "Divine Dominion say WrathLander have never beaten them outright! The new champions accepted their challenge for Next Wednesday!" [AEW posted on X](https://x.com/AEW/status/2123582386675790287) on Thursday night, confirming a Women's World Tag Team Title match for the Thanksgiving Eve edition of Dynamite.

## The match

Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander, known as WrathLander, will defend against Megan Bayne and Lena Kross, the team called Divine Dominion. According to the [Wrestling Observer](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/andrade-kyle-fletcher-aew-dynamite-next-week/), it is WrathLander's first defence since winning the titles, and Divine Dominion are the former champions. The match was set up in a backstage segment on Wednesday's show.

## Why it is personal

The challengers' line is pointed. AEW's post says Divine Dominion claim WrathLander "have never beaten them outright." In other words, the champions took the belts without pinning the team now coming for them, which gives Bayne and Kross a ready made argument that the title change does not settle anything.

For WrathLander, a clean win would end that talk quickly. For Divine Dominion, it is a fast route back to the gold they lost.

## The rest of the show

The Observer says Dynamite comes from Omaha, Nebraska, next Wednesday, and also features the first ever singles match between Andrade El Idolo and Kyle Fletcher. Several Continental Classic matches are expected to be added, with the tournament already under way as covered in our [Blue League opener report](/articles/2026-11-19-bandido-beats-hangman-page-continental-classic-blue-league-dynamite-november-18/). The full tournament fields are in our [Continental Classic guide](/articles/2026-11-17-aew-continental-classic-2026-gold-blue-league-fields/).

Dynamite airs at 8pm Eastern on TBS and HBO Max, AEW says.
