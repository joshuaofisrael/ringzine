Ilja Dragunov has his first championship in AEW. He beat Tommaso Ciampa for the AEW National Championship at Full Gear in Phoenix on Saturday, [F4W Online reports](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/result-of-national-title-match-at-aew-full-gear-2026/), in what was his first AEW pay per view match and only his third match for the company.

## How it ended

Both men kicked out of several big moves. In the closing stretch, Dragunov countered Ciampa's Fujiwara armbar attempt into a powerbomb, then hit the D Bomb for the pin, according to F4W. [Cageside Seats](https://www.cagesideseats.com/aew/486880/aew-full-gear-2026-results-ilja-dragunov-wins-national-title-tommaso-ciampa-very-short-reign) notes that Dragunov also landed a D Bomb from the ring apron earlier in the match.

## A short reign

Ciampa had won the title from Andrade El Ídolo on the 7 November edition of Collision, which Cageside Seats says took place on Chris Jericho's cruise, so his run lasted just seven days. F4W points out that his TNT Championship reign earlier this year lasted only 11 days. Cageside Seats says Dragunov challenged Ciampa after Ciampa called himself the greatest signing in AEW history.

[AEW's own results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-full-gear-results-november-14-2026) list the National title as one of three championships to change hands at Full Gear, along with the World Trios titles and the Women's World Tag Team titles.
