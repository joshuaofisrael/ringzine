**Stephanie Vaquer did not wrestle on Raw because she was not medically cleared, WWE said.** That part is confirmed. Why she was not cleared is not: WWE has given no reason, and the ankle injury explanation comes from outside reports, so this story carries a Rumor label.

## Confirmed by WWE

Vaquer was set to face Becky Lynch to decide the No. 1 contender for Liv Morgan's Women's World Championship. Raw General Manager Adam Pearce announced she was not medically cleared, and Lynch faced Morgan for the title instead, [according to WWE's Raw results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results). Lynch won by disqualification, so Morgan kept the title, [WWE reported](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).

[Fightful also reported](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/stephanie-vaquer-not-cleared-becky-lynch-granted-womens-world-title-match/) that Pearce opened the show with the announcement.

## Reported, not confirmed by WWE

[Wrestling Inc reported](https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2297807/stephanie-vaquer-unavailable-wwe-raw-no-1-contender-becky-lynch/) that Vaquer reportedly suffered a legitimate ankle injury in the Money in the Bank triple threat match where Morgan won the title back. The same report says she wrestled at WWE live events in the days after that show, and that initial reports did not expect her to miss significant time.

Wrestling Inc also reported that Pearce had said on social media over the weekend that Vaquer was cleared, before WWE posted on X hours before Raw that questions had arisen about her medical availability, [per Wrestling Inc](https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2297838/wwe-raw-stephanie-vaquer-not-cleared-becky-lynch-womens-title-liv-morgan/).

## What we do not know

WWE has not said what is keeping Vaquer out, how long she will be out, or whether this is connected to the injury reported after Money in the Bank. RingZine will update this story and its label if WWE gives more detail.
