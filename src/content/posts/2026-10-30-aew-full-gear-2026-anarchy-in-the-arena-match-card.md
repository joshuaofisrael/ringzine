**The Death Riders and The Cut are headed for Anarchy in the Arena at AEW Full Gear.** Jon Moxley issued the challenge on Fright Night Dynamite after The Cut beat him and Daniel Garcia in the opening tag match, [AEW's results page said](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026).

Full Gear takes place on Saturday, November 14, at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona, live on pay per view.

## How the match came together

Jay White pinned Garcia with the Blade Runner after Clark Connors ran in, according to AEW. The two groups brawled after the bell, and Moxley took the microphone and called for "Anarchy in the Arena" at Full Gear.

Later in the show, The Cut ambushed the Death Riders again. In that storyline segment, AEW said White hit Claudio Castagnoli with a Blade Runner on the exposed floor. Team lineups for the Full Gear match have not been announced, [Wrestling Online reported](https://www.wrestling-online.com/aew/anarchy-in-the-arena-match-announced-for-full-gear-2026/).

## What Anarchy in the Arena is

The match is a no disqualification brawl between two teams that can spill all over the building, and it ends by pinfall or submission. Wrestling Online noted every previous edition since 2022 was held at Double or Nothing, so this is the first time the match moves to Full Gear. The Death Riders also took part in last year's version.

## Full Gear 2026 card so far

This list combines AEW's Dynamite results with the card [compiled by SEScoops](https://www.sescoops.com/article/main-event-confirmed-for-aew-full-gear).

- **AEW World Championship:** Will Ospreay (c) vs. Kyle Fletcher
- **AEW World Tag Team Championship:** The Young Bucks (c) vs. Mark Davis and Jake Doyle
- **AEW World Trios Championship:** Hangman Page, Brody King and Bandido (c) vs. Swerve Strickland, Austin Creed and Kofi
- **AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship:** The Brawling Birds (c) vs. Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander
- **Anarchy in the Arena:** Death Riders vs. The Cut

## Still unsettled

Swerve Strickland wants the trios match turned into a Texas Death Match, and Creed and Kofi said on Dynamite they did not want that stipulation, AEW reported. No change has been made official.
