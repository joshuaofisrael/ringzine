**Chad Gable pinned CM Punk in a non title match and Sami Zayn retained the Undisputed WWE Championship over Cody Rhodes and Kevin Owens at WWE's Thursday live event in Youngstown, Ohio,** [Fightful reported](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/wwe-live-results-10-29-26-chad-gable-scores-pinfall-victory-over-cm-punk/). The show was part of the Road to Survivor Series: WarGames tour at the Covelli Centre.

WWE does not post results from untelevised live events, so this list comes from outlet reports, which is why it carries a rumor label. Fightful and [PWMania, citing WrestlingBodySlam.com](https://www.pwmania.com/wwe-road-to-survivor-series-results-from-youngstown-ohio-10-29-2026), published matching results.

## Results

- **WWE United States Championship:** Trick Williams (c) beat Damian Priest
- Chelsea Green and Tiffany Stratton beat Nia Jax and Lash Legend. PWMania said Green pinned Jax.
- Gunther beat Finn Bálor with a rollup, per PWMania.
- **WWE Women's Tag Team Championship:** Michin and B Fab (c) beat Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley in a Halloween costume match. PWMania said Jade Cargill interfered.
- Oba Femi, LA Knight and Solo Sikoa beat Bronson Reed, Bron Breakker and Austin Theory. PWMania said Femi pinned Theory.
- **Trick or Treat Street Fight:** Danhausen beat Kit Wilson
- **Non title match:** Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable beat CM Punk by pinfall
- **Undisputed WWE Championship, triple threat:** Sami Zayn (c) beat Cody Rhodes and Kevin Owens. PWMania said Zayn pinned Owens.

## Why it is worth noting

Zayn and Williams both kept their titles on Thursday, one night before they meet for the Undisputed WWE Championship on SmackDown. Our [SmackDown preview](/articles/2026-10-30-wwe-smackdown-october-30-2026-preview/) has the full stakes.

Live event results do not change WWE's television storylines unless WWE references them on air.
