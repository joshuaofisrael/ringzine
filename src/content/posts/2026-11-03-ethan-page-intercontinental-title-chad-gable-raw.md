Ethan Page left the November 2 edition of [WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-11-02/results) Raw as Intercontinental Champion, defeating Chad Gable in one of the programme's clearest in-ring changes. WWE's official results identify the title switch and describe how both wrestlers worked over a body part in the ring: Page went after Gable's neck and shoulder, while Gable focused on Page's knee. The finish came after Page used an exposed turnbuckle and followed with an Egoplex.

The sequence matters because the result was not presented as a straightforward exchange of signature moves. WWE says Gable used a Dragon Screw to compromise Page's leg and applied an Ankle Lock, while also delivering an Angle Slam onto the announce table. Those details show that the defending champion had a sustained route to victory. Page, however, was able to exploit Gable's neck issues at critical moments.

Gable also landed Chaos Theory, a move that would ordinarily give him an opportunity to secure a pin. According to WWE's account, the condition of his neck prevented him from maintaining the bridge needed to complete the cover. That created an opening for Page, who hit a Twisted Grin during the closing sequence before forcing Gable into the exposed corner. WWE records the subsequent Egoplex as the decisive move.
