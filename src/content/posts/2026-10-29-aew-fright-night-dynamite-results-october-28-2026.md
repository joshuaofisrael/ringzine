**Queen Aminata is the new TBS Champion, and Darby Allin is still the TNT Champion, after AEW Fright Night Dynamite on Wednesday, October 28.** Aminata beat Persephone in the main event, and Allin beat Josh Alexander, [according to AEW's results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026). The show came from Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis and aired on TBS and HBO Max. [Sports Illustrated's results](https://www.si.com/fannation/wrestling/aew/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-booking-grades-darby-allin-defends-the-tnt-title) list the same six winners.

## Full results

- **Queen Aminata def. Persephone** to win the TBS Championship with Off With Her Head. Britt Baker was at ringside and hit Aminata with the title belt before the finish. Aminata still got the pin. AEW called it her first AEW gold, and members of the women's locker room came out to celebrate, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026).
- **Darby Allin def. Josh Alexander** to retain the TNT Championship. Alexander attacked before the bell. Allin pinned him by falling back during an ankle lock. Steven Borden, wearing a clown mask, stopped a ringside run by RPG Vice, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026). After the match, Borden said he will team with the Young Bucks at Blood and Guts, and Allin said he wants Blood and Guts.
- **The Motor City Machine Guns def. FTR** in Match 3 of their best of five series. Alex Shelley pinned Dax Harwood. The Machine Guns now lead by two wins to one, and one more win takes the series, [AEW said](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026). After a handshake, Stokely threw powder at the Machine Guns and FTR attacked.
- **Kyle Fletcher def. Jungle Jack Perry** with an Avalanche Sheerdrop Brainbuster. Fletcher then said he wants Will Ospreay at Full Gear, and AEW wrote that Tony Khan made that world title match official, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026).
- **Jay White and David Finlay def. Jon Moxley and Daniel Garcia.** White pinned Garcia with a Blade Runner after Clark Connors interfered. Moxley then called for Anarchy in the Arena at Full Gear. In a later brawl, White hit Claudio Castagnoli with a Blade Runner on the exposed floor, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026).
- **Marina Shafir def. Rachel Armstrong** by submission with Mother's Milk. Shafir called out Mercedes Mone, Mone slapped her, and The Cut attacked Moxley at the commentary table, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-results-october-28-2026).

## Where the titles stand

Aminata closed the show as champion. Allin's TNT reign continues into Blood and Guts, which [AEW's Fright Night preview](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-dynamite-preview-october-28-2026) dated as November 4 in Jacksonville. The preview is the card RingZine posted before the show, in the [Fright Night preview](/articles/2026-10-28-aew-fright-night-dynamite-october-28-2026-preview/).

The Machine Guns lead the best of five series by two wins to one. FTR had tied it on the October 24 Collision. AEW also posted a Collision card and the Full Gear world title match during this show.
