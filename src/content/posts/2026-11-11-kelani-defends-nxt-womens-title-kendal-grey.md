WWE has announced that Kelani will defend the NXT Women's Championship against Kendal Grey on next week's NXT.

In a [post on X](https://x.com/WWE) on Tuesday night, shortly after NXT went off the air, the company wrote: "Set in STONE!! @kelani_wwe will defend the NXT Women's Title against @kendalgreywwe NEXT WEEK!"

That official post is the source for the booking. WWE did not, in the post, name a specific date beyond "next week" or describe how the challenge was made on the 10 November show. Next week's NXT is the usual Tuesday slot after this week's episode.

Kelani has held the title through recent NXT programming, including a defence against Paige earlier this month that this site has already covered. Kendal Grey is now the next official challenger named by WWE.

Title matches announced on social media can still be altered if NXT's producers change the card. As of WWE's Tuesday night post, Kelani versus Kendal Grey for the NXT Women's Championship is the advertised championship match for next week.
