AEW Full Gear 2026 is on Saturday, 14 November, at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, with Will Ospreay defending the AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher in the main event, [Yahoo Sports reports](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/article/aew-full-gear-2026-full-match-card-start-time-how-to-watch-will-ospreay-vs-kyle-fletcher-on-saturday-011152694.html). Wednesday's three hour Dynamite and Collision special was the last television before the show.

## Start time and how to watch

The main show starts at 8 p.m. ET, with the Zero Hour pre show at 7 p.m. ET, according to [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/article/aew-full-gear-2026-full-match-card-start-time-how-to-watch-will-ospreay-vs-kyle-fletcher-on-saturday-011152694.html). It reports the event is on pay per view through HBO Max in the United States for $39.99 and on MyAEW in other countries, with other providers varying by country.

## The card

Matches confirmed by [AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026) and listed by [the Wrestling Observer](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/aew-results/aew-dynamite-collision-live-results-november-11-2026/) and [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/article/aew-full-gear-2026-full-match-card-start-time-how-to-watch-will-ospreay-vs-kyle-fletcher-on-saturday-011152694.html):

- **AEW World Championship:** Will Ospreay (c) vs. Kyle Fletcher
- **AEW Women's World Championship:** Mercedes Moné (c) vs. Marina Shafir
- **AEW International Championship:** Kazuchika Okada (c) vs. Adam Copeland
- **AEW National Championship:** Tommaso Ciampa (c) vs. Ilja Dragunov
- **AEW World Trios Championship, Texas Death match:** Hangman Adam Page, Bandido and Brody King (c) vs. Swerve Strickland, Kofi and Austin Creed
- **AEW World Tag Team Championship:** The Young Bucks (c) vs. Mark Davis and Jake Doyle
- **AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship:** The Brawling Birds (c) vs. Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron
- **Anarchy in the Arena:** Death Riders and The Conglomeration vs. The Cut
- **Tailgate Brawl, TBS Championship three way:** Queen Aminata (c) vs. Persephone vs. Maya World

## Storylines to know

On Wednesday, Fletcher admitted he was jealous of Ospreay and slapped him at the end of their face to face segment, [the Wrestling Observer reports](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/aew-results/aew-dynamite-collision-live-results-november-11-2026/). The Motor City Machine Guns won their series with FTR and will face whoever leaves Full Gear with the tag titles, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026).

Cards can change before the show. This list reflects what AEW and the reports above had confirmed by Thursday morning.
