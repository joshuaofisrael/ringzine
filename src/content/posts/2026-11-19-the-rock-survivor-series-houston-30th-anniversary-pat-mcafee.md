The Rock will be in Houston for Survivor Series. "The Rock just announced on 'The Pat McAfee Show' that he'll be in Houston next weekend for Survivor Series," [WrestleVotes posted on X](https://x.com/WrestleVotes/status/2123116267309580418) on Wednesday afternoon.

## What he said

WWE confirmed it in [an article on WWE.com](https://www.wwe.com/article/survivor-series/the-rock-is-coming-to-survivor-series). "I would not miss next week for the world," The Rock told McAfee. "I'm going to Houston for Survivor Series and I can't wait."

WWE says this year's Survivor Series: WarGames marks the 30th anniversary of his WWE debut. The company has not said whether he will appear on the show itself or in what role, so for now all that is confirmed is that he will be in Houston.

## When and how to watch

Survivor Series: WarGames streams live on Saturday 28 November at 6pm ET (3pm PT) on ESPN with the Unlimited Plan, according to WWE. It is the 40th annual Survivor Series.

## The card so far

WWE lists these matches:

* The Bloodline against The 946 in the Men's WarGames Match
* Charlotte Flair, Stephanie Vaquer, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley and Bayley against Fatal Influence, Jade Cargill and Michin in the Women's WarGames Match
* Team USA against Team Mexico in a Traditional Survivor Series Match
* Women's World Champion Liv Morgan against Becky Lynch

For more on the traditional elimination match, see our story on [Rey Mysterio and Team Mexico](/articles/2026-11-07-rey-mysterio-team-mexico-survivor-series-2026/).
