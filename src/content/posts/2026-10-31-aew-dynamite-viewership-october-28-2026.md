**AEW's Halloween week Dynamite held above 600,000 viewers despite going head to head with the World Series.** Wrestlenomics reports the October 28 episode averaged 620,000 viewers on TBS, [Fightful said](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/aew-dynamite-10-28-viewership-drops-remains-over-600000-viewers-against-world-series/). AEW has not published its own figures, so treat these as reported numbers.

## The numbers

| Episode | Viewers on TBS | Adults 18 to 49 rating |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October 28 (Fright Night) | 620,000 | 0.14 |
| October 21 | 661,000 | 0.15 |

That is a drop of 41,000 viewers week to week, based on the two reported averages.

## The competition

The episode aired against Game 5 of the World Series between the Milwaukee Brewers and the Tampa Bay Rays, according to Fightful. Dynamite was also simulcast on HBO Max, and no viewership data is available for that stream, so the TBS figure does not capture the full audience.

[AEW's own recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-fright-night-collision-preview-october-31-2026) says Fright Night Dynamite saw Queen Aminata win the TBS Championship and Kyle Fletcher earn a shot at AEW World Champion Will Ospreay at Full Gear.
