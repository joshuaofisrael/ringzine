**Penta and Je'Von Evans will challenge Bron Breakker and Austin Theory for the World Tag Team Championship at WWE Crown Jewel on Saturday, November 7.** Raw General Manager Adam Pearce made the match after The Vision attacked Penta and Evans on the October 26 Raw, [WWE announced](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwe-crown-jewel/crown-jewel-2026/match-1-crown-jewel-2026).

## How to watch

WWE says Crown Jewel streams live at noon ET, 9 a.m. PT, on the ESPN App with an ESPN Unlimited plan in the United States, [per the match page](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwe-crown-jewel/crown-jewel-2026/match-1-crown-jewel-2026).

## How it got here

WWE's timeline on the [Crown Jewel match page](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwe-crown-jewel/crown-jewel-2026/match-1-crown-jewel-2026):

- On the September 21 Raw, Theory interfered when Evans faced Breakker.
- After Evans and Penta beat Dominik Mysterio and JD McDonagh the next week, Breakker speared both men.
- On the October 5 Raw, Evans used The Vision's brass knuckles on Theory to win a nontitle tag match.
- Penta won the Money in the Bank ladder match, and Evans later helped Penta beat Breakker on Raw.

On the October 26 Raw, Breakker stopped Penta from cashing in his contract on Sami Zayn, and Breakker and Bronson Reed then attacked Penta and Evans after Zayn's title defense, [according to WWE's results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).

## Also on the card

Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn faces World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns in a Champion vs. Champion match at the same event, [WWE said](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).
