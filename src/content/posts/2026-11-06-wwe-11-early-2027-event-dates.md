WWE announced 11 new event dates across North America to open 2027, including nine fresh Raw and SmackDown editions.

WWE said on X on 5 November 2026 that tickets for each event go on sale Wednesday, 11 November, at 10am local time. The matching [WWE.com article](https://www.wwe.com/article/wwe-announces-11-new-event-dates-to-kick-off-2027) lists a fan presale starting Tuesday, 10 November, and points buyers to WWE.com/Events.

## The dates WWE published

Per WWE.com, the slate is:

- Friday 1 January: St. Paul, Minn., Grand Casino Arena, WWE Holiday Tour
- Monday 4 January: San Antonio, Frost Bank Center, Monday Night Raw
- Friday 8 January: Memphis, FedExForum, Friday Night SmackDown
- Monday 11 January: Chicago, United Center, Monday Night Raw
- Friday 29 January: Portland, Moda Center, Friday Night SmackDown
- Saturday 30 January: Vancouver, Rogers Arena, Road to Royal Rumble
- Monday 1 February: Seattle, Climate Pledge Arena, Monday Night Raw
- Monday 8 February: Anaheim, Honda Center, Monday Night Raw
- Friday 12 February: Dallas, American Airlines Center, Friday Night SmackDown
- Monday 15 February: Atlanta, State Farm Arena, Monday Night Raw
- Monday 22 February: Indianapolis, Gainbridge Fieldhouse, Monday Night Raw

That calendar mixes weekly TV taping cities with a Holiday Tour opener and a Vancouver Road to Royal Rumble stop. RingZine will update if WWE adds card details or changes on sale windows.
