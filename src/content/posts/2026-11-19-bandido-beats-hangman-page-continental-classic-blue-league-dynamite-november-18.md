Bandido is first on the board in the Continental Classic Blue League, and he got there by beating his own trios partner. According to [Sports Illustrated's Dynamite results](https://www.si.com/fannation/wrestling/aew/aew-dynamite-results-nov18), the ROH World Champion pinned Hangman Adam Page with the 21 Plex on Wednesday 18 November to earn three points.

[Fightful](https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/aew-dynamite-results-11-18-2026-hangman-page-vs-bandido-tbs-title-match-mjf-mercedes-mone-more/) says the show came live from the Mission Ballroom in Denver, Colorado, and that Bandido vs Page headlined it.

## How Bandido won

Sports Illustrated says Page's death match wound from Full Gear opened up during the match and he was soon covered in blood. Near the finish, Bandido countered the Buckshot Lariat and tried to roll Page into a pin, but Page kicked out at two. Bandido then hit a knee strike in the style of a GTS and set up the 21 Plex for the win.

[Slam Wrestling](https://slamwrestling.net/news/aew-dynamite-results-november-18-2026/) describes plenty of back and forth between the former Trios champions, including a dive to the floor from Bandido and a powerbomb from Page for a near fall.

## The handshake

After the match Page at first brushed off Bandido's offer of a handshake. Sports Illustrated says both men then knelt and bowed their heads to each other before shaking hands as the show went off the air.

## The rest of the Continental Classic so far

In the Gold League, Orange Cassidy beat Jon Moxley and Nick Wayne beat Mike Bailey, according to Sports Illustrated. Elsewhere on the show Queen Aminata kept the TBS Championship in a four way match against AZM, Maya World and Mina Shirakawa, and Samoa Joe beat Jack Perry.
