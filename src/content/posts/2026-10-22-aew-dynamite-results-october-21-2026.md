**Ilja Dragunov won his AEW in ring debut over "Speedball" Mike Bailey in the Dynamite main event, and the Don Callis Family accepted a men's Blood and Guts match for November 4 in Jacksonville**, according to [AEW's official Dynamite results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026). The show aired live from Coral Gables, Florida.

## Full results

- **Mark Davis and Jake Doyle def. the Young Bucks** in an AEW World Tag Team Championship eliminator in under three minutes, earning a future title shot, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026).
- **Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight def. Will Ospreay and Adam Copeland.** Fletcher pinned Copeland with a Sheerdrop Brainbuster after Copeland crashed into the International Championship held by Kazuchika Okada, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026).
- **Swerve Strickland def. Tome Filip** almost immediately with a Vertebreaker.
- **Jay White and Orange Cassidy def. Wheeler Yuta and Daniel Garcia** when White hit Yuta with the Blade Runner.
- **Jon Moxley def. Roderick Strong** in an AEW Continental Championship eliminator by rear naked choke.
- **Queen Aminata def. Maya World** to become the No. 1 contender for Persephone's TBS Championship.
- **Ilja Dragunov def. Mike Bailey** in the main event.

All results are from [AEW's recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026).

## Blood and Guts

After the tag match, the Callis Family attacked Ospreay and Copeland until Darby Allin and the returning Steven Borden made the save. Borden challenged the Callis Family to Blood and Guts, and Don Callis later accepted. AEW lists the match as Ospreay, Copeland, Borden, the Young Bucks, and Allin against Okada, Fletcher, Wardlow, Knight, Davis, and Doyle, [per the recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026).

## New alliance

After Moxley's win, The Dogs and the Bang Bang Gang joined forces to attack the Death Riders and The Conglomeration, and White hit Cassidy with the Blade Runner, [AEW reported](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026).

## What is next

Aminata faces Persephone for the TBS Championship on Fright Night Dynamite. Swerve Strickland and The New Level get their AEW World Trios Championship rematch against Hangman Page and Brodido at Full Gear, but Page refused Swerve's demand to make it a Texas Death match. AEW Full Gear is November 14 in Phoenix, [per AEW](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-results-october-21-2026).
