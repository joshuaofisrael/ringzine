**WWE Crown Jewel streams live on Saturday, November 7, at noon ET and 9 a.m. PT from Riyadh, with four matches announced so far**, [according to WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwe-crown-jewel/crown-jewel-2026). In the United States it streams on the ESPN App with an ESPN Unlimited plan, and on Netflix internationally.

## Announced card

- **Crown Jewel Championship:** World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns vs. Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn
- **Women's Crown Jewel Championship:** Women's World Champion Liv Morgan vs. WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green
- **Last Man Standing:** Cody Rhodes vs. Randy Orton
- **World Tag Team Championship:** Bron Breakker and Austin Theory (c) vs. Penta and Je'Von Evans

## The October 30 catch

Both SmackDown champions still have to get through title defenses first. Zayn defends the Undisputed WWE Championship against Trick Williams on the October 30 SmackDown, and Green defends the WWE Women's Championship against Nikki Bella on the same show, [CBS Sports reported](https://www.cbssports.com/wwe/news/wwe-crown-jewel-card-matches-2026/). If either title changes hands, the new champion takes that Crown Jewel spot.

Morgan is already locked in after winning the Women's World Championship at Money in the Bank, and she would be the first Crown Jewel champion to win twice, per the same report.

## Background

WWE's preview notes that Zayn once stood with Reigns in the original Bloodline before they fell out. Rhodes and Orton headlined WrestleMania this year, and their feud continues in a Last Man Standing match. Penta and Evans earned their tag title shot after The Vision attacked them on Raw, as [RingZine covered](/articles/2026-10-27-penta-evans-tag-title-match-crown-jewel/).
