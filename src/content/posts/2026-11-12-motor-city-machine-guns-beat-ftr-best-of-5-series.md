The Motor City Machine Guns have won their Best of 5 series with FTR. Alex Shelley rolled up Dax Harwood for the three count as time was running out in Match 5 on Wednesday's Dynamite, giving Shelley and Chris Sabin a 3 to 2 series win, [according to AEW's official results](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026).

The deciding match was set up last week, when [Tony Khan booked a fifth and final match](/articles/2026-11-11-tony-khan-books-ftr-mcmg-best-of-5-decider/) with the series level at two wins each.

## How it ended

AEW's recap says the one minute warning had been called and FTR were going for the Shatter Machine with about 30 seconds left. Shelley tried a sunset flip on Harwood, kicked Cash Wheeler out of the ring when he tried to help, then rolled Harwood up for the win [at the buzzer](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026). The [Wrestling Observer's live report](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/aew-results/aew-dynamite-collision-live-results-november-11-2026/) also has Shelley pinning Harwood, and says the two teams shook hands in the ring afterwards before FTR and Stokely left.

## What the win is worth

AEW says the result makes the Motor City Machine Guns the number one contenders for the AEW World Tag Team Championship. They will face whichever team holds the titles after Full Gear, where the Young Bucks defend against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle, [per the same recap](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-collision-results-november-11-2026).

AEW also posted a short clip on X after the show in which the team said, ["this is just the beginning"](https://x.com/AEW).

Full Gear takes place this Saturday, 14 November, in Phoenix. No date has been announced yet for the Machine Guns' title shot.
