**No titles changed hands on Raw.** Sami Zayn kept the Undisputed WWE Championship by beating Je'Von Evans, and Liv Morgan kept the Women's World Championship because Becky Lynch won their main event by disqualification, according to [WWE's official Raw results](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).

## Full results

- **Sami Zayn def. Je'Von Evans** to retain the Undisputed WWE Championship. Penta came out to cash in his Money in the Bank contract, but Bron Breakker attacked him at ringside. Evans jumped out of the ring to go after Breakker, and Zayn used the distraction to win with the Helluva Kick, [WWE reported](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results). Breakker and Bronson Reed then attacked Penta and Evans.
- **Bayley def. Lyra Valkyria** in a No Disqualification match. Bayley put Valkyria through a table at ringside with a flying elbow, then won with a Kendo stick crossface, [per WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).
- **IYO SKY def. Raquel Rodriguez** in a nontitle match by countering the Tejana Bomb into a pin. Rodriguez, the Women's Intercontinental Champion, and Roxanne Perez attacked SKY after the bell, [WWE said](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).
- **Ethan Page and Big Cass def. Chad Gable and Dragon Lee.** Gable was taken out at ringside, and Page pinned Lee after a big boot from Cass and the Egoplex, [according to WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).
- **Becky Lynch def. Liv Morgan by disqualification** in a Women's World Championship match, so Morgan keeps the title. The Judgment Day attacked while Lynch had Morgan in her submission hold, which forced the referee to stop the match, [WWE reported](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).

## Why Lynch got the title shot

Lynch was scheduled to face Stephanie Vaquer for a No. 1 contender spot. Raw General Manager Adam Pearce announced that Vaquer was not medically cleared, so Lynch went straight to a title match with Morgan, [per WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).

## The Stratton tease

After the main event, Chelsea Green and IYO SKY ran off The Judgment Day. Tiffany Stratton, who holds the women's Money in the Bank contract, then nearly cashed in on Morgan before Dominik Mysterio pulled Morgan to safety, [WWE said](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results).

## What is next

Zayn faces World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns in a Champion vs. Champion match at Crown Jewel, [according to WWE](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-10-26/results). After the attack on Penta and Evans, Pearce gave them a World Tag Team Championship match against Bron Breakker and Austin Theory at Crown Jewel on Saturday, November 7, [WWE announced](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwe-crown-jewel/crown-jewel-2026/match-1-crown-jewel-2026).
