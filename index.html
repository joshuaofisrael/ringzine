Latest

  • Confirmed WWE November 21, 2026

    Kevin Owens finally beat GUNTHER, and then Sami Zayn ruined his night with one kick

    On the 20 November SmackDown, Kevin Owens beat GUNTHER after Finn Bálor's low blow, then Sami Zayn blindsided him with a Helluva Kick. Jade Cargill also beat Charlotte Flair to earn a WarGames advantage.

  • Confirmed WWE November 21, 2026

    Team USA lost two fifth-man options in one night, and Cody Rhodes says he will reveal the last one on Raw

    On the 20 November SmackDown, "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan backed out of joining Team USA and Damian Priest hurt Trick Williams's knee with the ring bell, which WWE says takes him out of Survivor Series. Cody Rhodes will reveal the final member on Monday's Raw.

  • Confirmed WWE November 20, 2026

    Team USA basketball coach Kara Lawson says she is "dead serious" about a WWE match, and has a message for Triple H

    Duke and Team USA women's basketball head coach Kara Lawson sat front row at Monday's Raw in Raleigh on tickets from Charlotte Flair, and now says she wants to wrestle in WWE.

  • Confirmed WWE November 20, 2026

    Nick Khan admits WWE wants its best seats priced "as high as possible", and says cheap tickets just feed the resellers

    Speaking at Bloomberg's Screentime conference, WWE President Nick Khan said the company pushes its most expensive tickets as high as it can and stops short of truly family friendly pricing upstairs because brokers would buy those seats.

  • Confirmed AEW November 20, 2026

    Divine Dominion say WrathLander have never beaten them, and they get the chance to prove it for the titles on Thanksgiving Eve

    New AEW Women's World Tag Team Champions Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander will make their first defence against former champions Megan Bayne and Lena Kross on the Thanksgiving Eve Dynamite in Omaha next Wednesday.

  • Confirmed AEW November 19, 2026

    Ilja Dragunov told Don Callis no, so his first National title defence is this Saturday against El Clon

    New AEW National Champion Ilja Dragunov turned down an offer to join the Don Callis Family on Dynamite and offered to defend against any member. Callis picked El Clon, and the match is official for Saturday's Collision.

  • Confirmed AEW November 19, 2026

    MJF says he is the real AEW World Champion, and wants Will Ospreay in a title vs title match at Worlds End

    On Dynamite, MJF challenged Will Ospreay to a match at Worlds End for both versions of the AEW World Championship. AEW has not made it official yet.

  • Confirmed WWE November 19, 2026

    WWE president Nick Khan names Oba Femi the company's next big thing, and credits The Undertaker for finding him

    Khan says WWE stopped looking for the next John Cena and started recruiting college athletes. The Undertaker's campus visits brought in Femi, a former University of Alabama shot putter Khan says looks like no wrestler before him.

  • Confirmed WWE November 19, 2026

    "I would not miss next week for the world": The Rock says he is coming to Survivor Series in Houston

    The Rock told The Pat McAfee Show he will be in Houston for Survivor Series: WarGames on Saturday 28 November, the 30th anniversary of his WWE debut. WWE has not said whether he will appear on the show itself.

  • Confirmed AEW November 19, 2026

    Bandido beats his own trios partner Hangman Page with the 21 Plex to open the Continental Classic Blue League

    The ROH World Champion took the first three points in the Blue League on Wednesday's Dynamite in Denver, countering the Buckshot Lariat before landing the 21 Plex. Page, bleeding again from his Full Gear wound, eventually shook his hand.

  • Confirmed WWE November 18, 2026

    Kelani Jordan keeps the NXT Women's Championship by getting herself disqualified against Kendal Grey

    Grey won the match on Tuesday's NXT after Jordan slapped the referee while trapped in the Ankle Lock, but the title does not change hands on a disqualification. It was Grey's first shot at the belt she lost to Jordan.

  • Confirmed WWE November 18, 2026

    Zilla Fatu beats DarkState's Dion Lennox on NXT after Los Perros del Mal crash the match

    Fatu crashed into the ring steps when Lennox moved, but a brawl between Los Perros del Mal and the rest of DarkState gave him the opening for a frog splash and the pin.

  • Confirmed WWE November 17, 2026

    IYO SKY beats Raquel Rodriguez to win the Women's Intercontinental Championship on Raw

    WWE announced the title change on X during Monday's Raw. The 946 also earned the advantage in the Men's WarGames Match, and LA Knight beat The Miz to join Team USA for Survivor Series.

  • Confirmed AEW November 17, 2026

    AEW reveals the 2026 Continental Classic leagues: Moxley and Ciampa in Gold, Page and Ricochet in Blue

    Twelve wrestlers are split into two round robin leagues of six. Semifinals and the final are set for AEW Worlds End in Minneapolis on 19 December, under Continental Rules with a 20 minute time limit.

  • Confirmed AEW November 16, 2026

    Tony Khan says three private planes saved the Full Gear card and confirms Persephone is signed for years

    At the post show media scrum, the AEW president said chartered flights got talent to Phoenix after Friday's FAA ground stop. He also said Persephone will stay beyond 2026 and claimed Full Gear pay per view buys rose year on year.

  • Confirmed AEW November 16, 2026

    Tony Khan confirms the Kasper Brothers, formerly the Creed Brothers, are All Elite after their Full Gear debut

    Jacob and Drew Kasper attacked The Young Bucks and the Motor City Machine Guns in Phoenix, then Khan posted that the pair are signed. Their WWE run ended in early November.

  • Confirmed AEW November 15, 2026

    AEW Full Gear 2026: Ospreay retains against Fletcher, then MJF returns and attacks him

    Will Ospreay kept the AEW World Championship in a 41 minute main event in Phoenix before a masked MJF ambushed him and held up his own title belt. AEW says MJF will speak on Dynamite in Denver on Wednesday.

  • Confirmed AEW November 15, 2026

    Ilja Dragunov wins the AEW National Championship at Full Gear, ending Ciampa's reign after seven days

    Dragunov beat Tommaso Ciampa with a D Bomb in his first AEW pay per view match to claim his first title in the company, a week after Ciampa won the belt from Andrade.

  • Confirmed AEW November 15, 2026

    Mercedes Moné keeps the AEW Women's World title at Full Gear with her feet on the ropes

    Moné survived Marina Shafir's Brutalizer and won with a cradle while using the ropes for leverage, leaving the Death Riders member with a clear claim to a rematch.

  • Confirmed WWE November 14, 2026

    SmackDown results 13 November: women's WarGames teams set, Rey Fénix joins Team Mexico, Cena invites Charlotte

    Charlotte Flair's team will face Fatal Influence, Jade Cargill and Michin inside WarGames at Survivor Series, John Cena added Charlotte and Fatal Influence to his Classic, and Gunther beat Finn Bálor.

Questions

What does RingZine cover?

RingZine covers WWE and AEW: weekly show results, premium live events and pay per views, title changes, injuries, and roster news.

What do the Confirmed and Rumor labels mean?

Confirmed means an official WWE or AEW source, such as the company's own results page or an on air announcement, backs the story. Rumor means a named outlet reported it and the company has not confirmed it. The full rule is in the editorial policy.

Does RingZine use WWE or AEW photos?

No. Pictures on RingZine are original graphic title cards made for the site. We do not use promotion footage or photographs. Details are on the disclaimer page.

How does RingZine correct an error?

A factual error is fixed on the article with a note saying what changed, and the Last updated date moves. The policy is on the Corrections page.

How do I contact RingZine?

Use the contact form. We do not publish a street address.
