Questions

What does RingZine cover?

RingZine covers WWE and AEW: weekly show results, premium live events and pay per views, title changes, injuries, and roster news.

What do the Confirmed and Rumor labels mean?

Confirmed means an official WWE or AEW source, such as the company's own results page or an on air announcement, backs the story. Rumor means a named outlet reported it and the company has not confirmed it. The full rule is in the editorial policy.

Does RingZine use WWE or AEW photos?

No. Pictures on RingZine are original graphic title cards made for the site. We do not use promotion footage or photographs. Details are on the disclaimer page.

How does RingZine correct an error?

A factual error is fixed on the article with a note saying what changed, and the Last updated date moves. The policy is on the Corrections page.

How do I contact RingZine?

Use the contact form. We do not publish a street address.
